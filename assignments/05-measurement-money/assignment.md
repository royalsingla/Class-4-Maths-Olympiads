15 questions, 4-option MCQ, untimed.

---

**Q1.** Convert 5 m into centimetres.
A) 500 cm  B) 50 cm  C) 5000 cm  D) 5 cm

**Q2.** Convert 2,500 g into kilograms and grams.
A) 2 kg 500 g  B) 25 kg  C) 2 kg 50 g  D) 250 kg

**Q3.** Convert 3 L 200 mL into millilitres.
A) 3200 mL  B) 3020 mL  C) 320 mL  D) 3002 mL

**Q4.** 4 km 300 m + 2 km 800 m = ?
A) 6 km 100 m  B) 7 km 100 m  C) 7 km 900 m  D) 6 km 900 m

**Q5.** 10 kg − 3 kg 400 g = ?
A) 6 kg 600 g  B) 7 kg 400 g  C) 6 kg 400 g  D) 7 kg 600 g

**Q6.** How many millilitres make 1 litre?
A) 100  B) 10  C) 1000  D) 10000

**Q7.** How many grams make 1 kilogram?
A) 10  B) 100  C) 1000  D) 10000

**Q8.** A bottle contains 1 L 500 mL of juice. If 750 mL is poured out, how much juice is left?
A) 750 mL  B) 650 mL  C) 850 mL  D) 700 mL

**Q9.** A sack of rice weighs 25 kg. If 8 kg 250 g is used, how much rice is left?
A) 16 kg 750 g  B) 17 kg 250 g  C) 16 kg 250 g  D) 17 kg 750 g

**Q10.** Aarav has 3 notes of ₹100 and 5 coins of ₹10. How much money does he have in total?
A) ₹350  B) ₹300  C) ₹355  D) ₹305

**Q11.** A pen costs ₹18 and a notebook costs ₹45. What is the total cost of 2 pens and 1 notebook?
A) ₹81  B) ₹63  C) ₹99  D) ₹71

**Q12.** Meena bought a toy for ₹240 and gave the shopkeeper a ₹500 note. How much change will she get back?
A) ₹260  B) ₹250  C) ₹270  D) ₹240

**Q13.** A jug holds 2 L of water. How many 250 mL glasses can be completely filled from this jug?
A) 6  B) 7  C) 8  D) 10

**Q14.** An empty basket weighs 500 g. Filled with apples, it weighs 4 kg 500 g. What is the weight of the apples alone?
A) 4 kg  B) 4 kg 500 g  C) 3 kg 500 g  D) 5 kg

**Q15.** A shopkeeper has 3 sacks of sugar, each weighing 15 kg 500 g. What is the total weight of sugar he has?
A) 45 kg 500 g  B) 46 kg 500 g  C) 46 kg  D) 45 kg
