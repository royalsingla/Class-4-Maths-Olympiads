# Week 5 — Measurement & Money — Answer Key

**Q1. A) 500 cm** — 1 m = 100 cm, so 5 m = 500 cm.

**Q2. A) 2 kg 500 g** — 2500 g = 2000 g + 500 g = 2 kg 500 g.

**Q3. A) 3200 mL** — 3 L = 3000 mL; 3000 + 200 = 3200 mL.

**Q4. B) 7 km 100 m** — 300 m + 800 m = 1100 m = 1 km 100 m; 4 km + 2 km + 1 km 100 m = 7 km 100 m.

**Q5. A) 6 kg 600 g** — 10 kg = 9 kg 1000 g; 9 kg 1000 g − 3 kg 400 g = 6 kg 600 g.

**Q6. C) 1000** — 1 L = 1000 mL.

**Q7. C) 1000** — 1 kg = 1000 g.

**Q8. A) 750 mL** — 1500 mL − 750 mL = 750 mL.

**Q9. A) 16 kg 750 g** — 25000 g − 8250 g = 16750 g = 16 kg 750 g.

**Q10. A) ₹350** — 3 × ₹100 = ₹300; 5 × ₹10 = ₹50; total = ₹350.

**Q11. A) ₹81** — 2 pens = 2 × 18 = ₹36; + 1 notebook (₹45) = ₹81.

**Q12. A) ₹260** — ₹500 − ₹240 = ₹260.

**Q13. C) 8** — 2000 mL ÷ 250 mL = 8 glasses.

**Q14. A) 4 kg** — 4 kg 500 g − 500 g = 4 kg (4000 g).

**Q15. B) 46 kg 500 g** — 15 kg 500 g × 3 = 46500 g = 46 kg 500 g.
