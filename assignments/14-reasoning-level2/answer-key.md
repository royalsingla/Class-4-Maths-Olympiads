# Logical Reasoning — Level 2 — Answer Key

1. **A) Deepa, Aman, Chirag, Bina, Farid, Esha** — Deepa is at the left end, position 1. Chirag is third from the left, position 3. Bina is immediately right of Chirag, position 4. Esha is at the right end, position 6. Farid sits between Bina (4) and Esha (6), so Farid is position 5. The only person left, Aman, takes position 2.
2. **A) PQKN** — Shift each letter 2 steps forward: L→N, I→K, O→Q, N→P, giving "NKQP"; then reverse it: "PQKN".
3. **A) Father** — Rakesh is Meena's father, and Meena and Sohan are siblings, so Rakesh is Sohan's father too (this holds regardless of Sohan's gender).
4. **A) Priya** — The order from lowest to highest is Tina, Rohan, Priya, Sita, so Priya scored second-highest.
5. **D) STEP** — STOP, SPOT, and TOPS are all made of the same four letters (S, T, O, P) rearranged; STEP has an E instead of an O, so it isn't an anagram of the other three.
6. **A) 7-18-10** — D is the 4th letter (4+3=7), O is the 15th letter (15+3=18), G is the 7th letter (7+3=10).
7. **C) Sunday** — From 15th March to 10th April is (31−15) + 10 = 26 days later. 26 = 3 weeks + 5 days, so the day of the week moves forward 5 days from Tuesday: Sunday.
8. **D) 127** — Each term is double the previous term plus 1: 31×2+1 = 63, 63×2+1 = 127.
9. **A) Bela, Delhi** — Chetan likes Cherry. Since Aarav doesn't like Apple, Aarav must like Banana, leaving Apple for Bela. The Banana-liker lives in Agra, so Aarav lives in Agra. Bela doesn't live in Pune, so (with Agra taken) Bela must live in Delhi, leaving Pune for Chetan.
10. **B) 25** — The rule is "square the number": 4² = 16, so 5² = 25.
11. **B) Grandfather** — Suresh is Anil's father, and Anil is Vikram's father, so Suresh is Vikram's grandfather.
12. **A) 5 m South** — Starting facing East: turning right means now facing South (5 m South); turning right again means now facing West (8 m West). The two East/West legs cancel (8 m each way), leaving a net 5 m South of the starting point.
13. **B) 15** — Total in the queue = people ahead + people behind + Rani herself = 8 + 6 + 1 = 15.
14. **C) Both I and II follow** — "All pens are pencils" and "All pencils are erasers" chain together to give "All pens are erasers" (Conclusion I). Since pens are a real category here, it also follows that at least some erasers are pens (Conclusion II).
15. **C) 12** — Replace each symbol with its meaning: 10 **×** 5 **÷** 5 **+** 3 **−** 1. By order of operations: 10×5 = 50, 50÷5 = 10, then 10+3−1 = 12.

---

**How to use this:** 10+/15 is strong for a Level-2-focused set. The seating
puzzle (Q1), the logic grid (Q9), and the syllogism (Q14) are the three
hardest — if any of those tripped you up, re-read the clues one at a time
and write down what each one rules out before moving to the next, rather
than trying to hold the whole puzzle in your head.
