# Logical Reasoning — Level 2 (Compound Puzzles)

A dedicated harder follow-on to Weeks 9–10, for students who've qualified
Level 1. Every question here stacks two or more rules or clues together —
Level 1 tests one reasoning skill at a time, Level 2 combines them. 15
questions, 4-option MCQ, untimed.

---

**Q1.** Six friends — Aman, Bina, Chirag, Deepa, Esha, Farid — sit in a row facing north. Deepa sits at the left end. Chirag sits third from the left. Bina sits immediately to the right of Chirag. Esha sits at the right end. Farid sits between Bina and Esha. Find the seating order, left to right.
A) Deepa, Aman, Chirag, Bina, Farid, Esha
B) Deepa, Chirag, Aman, Bina, Farid, Esha
C) Aman, Deepa, Chirag, Bina, Farid, Esha
D) Deepa, Aman, Chirag, Farid, Bina, Esha

**Q2.** A word is coded by first moving each letter 2 steps forward in the alphabet, then reversing the order of the resulting letters. Using this rule, CAT becomes VCE. What does LION become?
A) PQKN  B) PQNK  C) QPKN  D) PKQN

**Q3.** Rakesh is Meena's father. Meena is Sohan's sister. How is Rakesh related to Sohan?
A) Father  B) Uncle  C) Grandfather  D) Brother

**Q4.** In a class test, Priya scored more than Rohan but less than Sita. Tina scored less than Rohan. Who scored the second-highest?
A) Priya  B) Rohan  C) Sita  D) Tina

**Q5.** Which word does NOT belong with the others?
A) STOP  B) SPOT  C) TOPS  D) STEP

**Q6.** In a code, each letter is replaced by its position number in the alphabet, and then 3 is added to each number (so B, the 2nd letter, becomes 2+3 = 5). What is the code for the word DOG?
A) 7-18-10  B) 7-17-10  C) 6-18-10  D) 7-18-11

**Q7.** 15th March is a Tuesday. What day of the week is 10th April of the same year?
A) Friday  B) Saturday  C) Sunday  D) Monday

**Q8.** Find the next number: 3, 7, 15, 31, 63, ___
A) 115  B) 120  C) 125  D) 127

**Q9.** Three friends — Aarav, Bela, Chetan — each like a different fruit (Apple, Banana, Cherry) and live in a different city (Delhi, Agra, Pune). Aarav does not like Apple. The person who likes Banana lives in Agra. Chetan likes Cherry. Bela does not live in Pune. Who likes Apple, and which city do they live in?
A) Bela, Delhi  B) Aarav, Agra  C) Chetan, Pune  D) Bela, Pune

**Q10.** 4 is to 16 as 5 is to ___.
A) 20  B) 25  C) 30  D) 10

**Q11.** Suresh's son is Anil. Anil's son is Vikram. How is Suresh related to Vikram?
A) Father  B) Grandfather  C) Uncle  D) Brother

**Q12.** Meena starts at her home facing East and walks 8 m. She then turns right and walks 5 m. She turns right again and walks 8 m. How far is she from her home now, and in which direction?
A) 5 m South  B) 5 m North  C) 8 m South  D) 13 m South

**Q13.** Rani is standing in a queue. There are 8 people ahead of her and 6 people behind her. How many people are there in the queue in total, including Rani?
A) 14  B) 15  C) 16  D) 17

**Q14.** Statements: All pens are pencils. All pencils are erasers. Conclusions: I. All pens are erasers. II. Some erasers are pens. Which conclusion(s) logically follow?
A) Only I follows  B) Only II follows  C) Both I and II follow  D) Neither follows

**Q15.** If + means ×, − means ÷, × means +, and ÷ means −, find the value of: 10 + 5 − 5 × 3 ÷ 1
A) 10  B) 11  C) 12  D) 14
