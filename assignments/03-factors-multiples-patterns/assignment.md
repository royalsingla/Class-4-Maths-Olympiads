# Week 3 — Factors, Multiples & Number Patterns

15 questions, 4-option MCQ, untimed.

---

**Q1.** Which of the following is a factor of 18?
A) 4  B) 5  C) 6  D) 8

**Q2.** Which of the following is a multiple of 7?
A) 30  B) 35  C) 40  D) 45

**Q3.** Find the HCF (highest common factor) of 12 and 18.
A) 4  B) 6  C) 9  D) 12

**Q4.** Find the LCM (lowest common multiple) of 4 and 6.
A) 10  B) 12  C) 24  D) 8

**Q5.** Which of these is a prime number?
A) 21  B) 27  C) 29  D) 33

**Q6.** How many factors does 12 have?
A) 4  B) 5  C) 6  D) 7

**Q7.** What is the smallest number (other than 0) that is a multiple of both 3 and 5?
A) 8  B) 10  C) 15  D) 20

**Q8.** Which of these numbers is NOT a multiple of 9?
A) 27  B) 36  C) 48  D) 63

**Q9.** Find the missing number in the pattern: 3, 6, 12, 24, ___
A) 36  B) 40  C) 48  D) 30

**Q10.** Find the missing number in the pattern: 100, 90, 80, ___, 60
A) 65  B) 70  C) 75  D) 72

**Q11.** Find the missing number in the pattern: 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, ___
A) 30  B) 32  C) 36  D) 49

**Q12.** Find the missing number in the pattern: 1, 3, 5, 7, ___, 11
A) 8  B) 9  C) 10  D) 13

**Q13.** Find the missing number in the pattern: 2, 4, 8, 16, ___, 64
A) 24  B) 28  C) 32  D) 30

**Q14.** Ritu is arranging 42 pencils into groups of 6. How many complete groups can she make, and how many pencils are left over?
A) 6 groups, 6 left  B) 7 groups, 0 left  C) 7 groups, 2 left  D) 8 groups, 0 left

**Q15.** Which of the following is both a factor of 24 and a multiple of 4?
A) 6  B) 8  C) 10  D) 16
