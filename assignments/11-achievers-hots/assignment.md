# Week 11 — Achievers / HOTS Mixed Practice

2-and-3-step problems pulling from every topic covered so far (numbers,
operations, fractions, measurement, geometry, patterns). 15 questions,
4-option MCQ, untimed. This is the hardest assignment in the plan —
don't worry if it takes longer or needs a second attempt.

---

**Q1.** A number when multiplied by 3 gives the same result as 24 added to the number. Find the number.
A) 12  B) 8  C) 24  D) 6

**Q2.** The sum of three consecutive numbers is 72. Find the largest number.
A) 23  B) 24  C) 25  D) 26

**Q3.** A rectangular field is 20 m long and 15 m wide. Find the cost of fencing it at ₹25 per metre.
A) ₹1,750  B) ₹1,700  C) ₹1,650  D) ₹1,800

**Q4.** Meena had some money. She spent 2/5 of it on a dress and was left with ₹360. How much money did she have at first?
A) ₹500  B) ₹600  C) ₹700  D) ₹900

**Q5.** A jug can hold 1 L 250 mL of water. How many such jugs are needed to fill a 10 L container completely?
A) 6  B) 7  C) 8  D) 10

**Q6.** The elder of two sisters is 4 years older than the younger. If the sum of their ages is 22, find the elder sister's age.
A) 11  B) 12  C) 13  D) 14

**Q7.** A shopkeeper had 500 kg of rice. He sold 3/10 of it on Monday and 1/5 of it on Tuesday. How much rice is left?
A) 200 kg  B) 250 kg  C) 300 kg  D) 350 kg

**Q8.** Find the next number in the pattern: 1, 2, 4, 7, 11, ___
A) 14  B) 15  C) 16  D) 18

**Q9.** A number leaves a remainder of 3 when divided by 5, and the quotient is 17. Find the number.
A) 85  B) 88  C) 90  D) 83

**Q10.** In a garden, there are 240 flowers. 1/4 of them are roses, 1/3 are marigolds, and the rest are lilies. How many lilies are there?
A) 80  B) 90  C) 100  D) 110

**Q11.** Four friends shared ₹960 such that each got ₹40 more than the previous one, with the first getting the least. If the first friend got ₹180, how much did the fourth friend get?
A) ₹280  B) ₹290  C) ₹300  D) ₹320

**Q12.** A square and a rectangle have the same perimeter of 48 cm. If the rectangle's length is 14 cm, find its breadth.
A) 8 cm  B) 10 cm  C) 12 cm  D) 6 cm

**Q13.** Rani is standing in a queue. There are 8 people ahead of her and 11 people behind her. How many people are there in the queue in total, including Rani?
A) 18  B) 19  C) 20  D) 21

**Q14.** A tank is filled using two pipes. Pipe A fills 250 L in an hour and Pipe B fills 180 L in an hour. If both pipes run together for 3 hours, how much water is in the tank?
A) 1,200 L  B) 1,290 L  C) 1,350 L  D) 1,400 L

**Q15.** The sum of the digits of a 2-digit number is 11. If the digits are reversed, the new number is 45 more than the original number. Find the original number.
A) 29  B) 38  C) 47  D) 56
