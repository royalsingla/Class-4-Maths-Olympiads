# Week 8 — Data Handling — Answer Key

**Q1. B) 4** — Four strokes represent the number 4.

**Q2. B) 5** — A bundled group (four strokes plus one diagonal across them) always stands for 5.

**Q3. B) 17** — 3 full groups × 5 = 15, plus 2 extra strokes = 17.

**Q4. B) 40** — Tuesday shows 4 symbols × 10 = 40 baskets.

**Q5. C) 30** — Thursday = 5 × 10 = 50; Wednesday = 2 × 10 = 20; difference = 50 − 20 = 30.

**Q6. C) 140** — Monday (30) + Tuesday (40) + Wednesday (20) + Thursday (50) = 140.

**Q7. D) Tina** — Tina scored 50, the highest in the table.

**Q8. C) Simmi** — Simmi scored 30, the lowest in the table.

**Q9. C) 200** — 40 + 35 + 45 + 30 + 50 = 200.

**Q10. B) 20** — Highest (50) − lowest (30) = 20.

**Q11. B) 15** — 40 − 25 = 15.

**Q12. C) White** — White has 20 cars, more than any other colour.

**Q13. C) 60** — 12 + 18 + 20 + 10 = 60.

**Q14. B) 30** — 6 × 5 = 30 books.

**Q15. C) 9** — 180 ÷ 20 = 9 symbols.
