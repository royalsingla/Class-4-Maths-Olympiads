# Week 8 — Data Handling

Tally marks, pictographs, and bar-graph-style tables. 15 questions,
4-option MCQ, untimed.

---

**Q1.** Four single tally strokes (||||, not yet grouped) represent which number?
A) 3  B) 4  C) 5  D) 6

**Q2.** In tally marks, one complete group (four vertical strokes crossed by a fifth diagonal stroke) stands for the number:
A) 4  B) 5  C) 6  D) 10

**Q3.** A count is recorded as 3 full tally groups and 2 extra strokes. What number does this represent?
A) 15  B) 17  C) 19  D) 20

The table below shows the number of fruit baskets sold by a shop over four
days, where **each 🍎 symbol = 10 baskets**:

| Day | Symbols |
|---|---|
| Monday | 🍎🍎🍎 |
| Tuesday | 🍎🍎🍎🍎 |
| Wednesday | 🍎🍎 |
| Thursday | 🍎🍎🍎🍎🍎 |

**Q4.** How many baskets were sold on Tuesday?
A) 30  B) 40  C) 35  D) 45

**Q5.** How many more baskets were sold on Thursday than on Wednesday?
A) 20  B) 25  C) 30  D) 35

**Q6.** What is the total number of baskets sold from Monday to Thursday?
A) 120  B) 130  C) 140  D) 150

The table below shows marks scored by 5 students in a Maths test (out of 50):

| Student | Marks |
|---|---|
| Aman | 40 |
| Priya | 35 |
| Rohan | 45 |
| Simmi | 30 |

**Q7.** Who scored the highest marks?
A) Aman  B) Priya  C) Rohan  D) Tina

**Q8.** Who scored the lowest marks?
A) Aman  B) Priya  C) Simmi  D) Rohan

**Q9.** What is the total marks scored by all 5 students?
A) 190  B) 195  C) 200  D) 210

**Q10.** What is the difference between the highest and lowest marks?
A) 15  B) 20  C) 25  D) 10

**Q11.** In a class of 40 students, 25 like Maths and the rest like English. How many like English?
A) 10  B) 15  C) 20  D) 25

**Q12.** Which colour is the most common?

**Q13.** What is the total number of cars in the parking lot?

**Q14.** If each symbol in a pictograph represents 5 books, and a shelf shows 6 symbols, how many books are on the shelf?
A) 25  B) 30  C) 35  D) 11
