# Fraction Interpretation — Answer Key

1. **A) 2/6** — 2 of the 6 equal parts are shaded, so the shaded fraction is 2/6.
2. **A) 3/4** — 3 of the 4 equal sectors are shaded, so the shaded fraction is 3/4.
3. **A) 4/10** — 4 of the 10 shapes are shaded, so the fraction shaded is 4/10.
4. **A) 5/8** — 5 of the 8 equal squares are shaded, so the shaded fraction is 5/8.
5. **A) 1/6** — 1 of the 6 equal sectors is shaded, so the shaded fraction is 1/6.
6. **B) 3/4** — 9 of the 12 shapes are shaded: 9/12. The greatest common factor of 9 and 12 is 3, so 9÷3=3 and 12÷3=4, giving 3/4.
7. **A) 12** — 3/5 of 20 = (20÷5)×3 = 4×3 = 12 ripe mangoes.
8. **A) 10** — 2/3 of 30 = 20 girls, so the remaining students are 30 − 20 = 10 boys.
9. **A) 11/4** — 2 whole = 2×4 = 8 quarters; 8 + 3 = 11, so 2 3/4 = 11/4.
10. **A) 3 2/5** — 17 ÷ 5 = 3 remainder 2, so 17/5 = 3 2/5.
11. **A) 7/10** — 7 of the 10 equal squares are shaded, so the shaded fraction is 7/10.
12. **A) 36** — 1/4 of 48 = 12 eaten, so 48 − 12 = 36 chocolates are left.
13. **A) 3/8** — There are 8 shapes in total and 3 of them are squares, so the fraction that are squares is 3/8.
14. **A) 5/8** — 8 − 3 = 5 slices are left out of 8, so the fraction left is 5/8.
15. **A** — A) 3 of 4 EQUAL parts shaded — that really is 3/4. B) only 2 of 4 parts shaded — that's 1/2, not 3/4. C) 3 of the 4 parts are shaded by COUNT, but the parts are NOT equal size, so the shaded area is much less than 3/4 even though 3 pieces are coloured in. D) 3 of 5 parts shaded — that's 3/5, a different total, not 3/4. Only the diagram with 3 of 4 truly EQUAL parts shaded correctly shows 3/4.

---

**How to use this:** for the diagram questions, always count BOTH the shaded parts and the total parts before writing the fraction. For the word problems, write down what 'the rest' or 'left over' means in each case — it's the most common place marks are lost.
