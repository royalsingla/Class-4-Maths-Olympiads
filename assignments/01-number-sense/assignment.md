# Week 1 — Number Sense & Numerals

Place value, number names, expanded form, ordering, rounding off, Roman
numerals, and simple number patterns. 15 questions, 4-option MCQ, untimed.

---

**Q1.** What is the place value of 7 in 57,342?
A) 7  B) 70  C) 700  D) 7000

**Q2.** What is the number name for 68,205?
A) Sixty-eight thousand two hundred five
B) Six thousand eight hundred and five
C) Sixty-eight thousand twenty-five
D) Six hundred eighty thousand five

**Q3.** Write 93,047 in expanded form.
A) 90000 + 3000 + 400 + 7
B) 90000 + 3000 + 40 + 7
C) 9000 + 300 + 40 + 7
D) 90000 + 300 + 47

**Q4.** Which is the greatest number?
A) 45,698  B) 45,986  C) 45,869  D) 45,689

**Q5.** Which is the smallest number?
A) 72,104  B) 72,014  C) 71,999  D) 72,100

**Q6.** Arrange in ascending order: 34521, 34251, 34125, 34512
A) 34125, 34251, 34512, 34521
B) 34521, 34512, 34251, 34125
C) 34125, 34512, 34251, 34521
D) 34251, 34125, 34521, 34512

A) 69998  B) 70000  C) 70001  D) 69990

**Q8.** The predecessor of 50,000 is:
A) 49999  B) 50001  C) 49990  D) 40999

**Q9.** Round off 6,742 to the nearest hundred.
A) 6700  B) 6800  C) 6750  D) 6000

**Q10.** Round off 3,256 to the nearest thousand.
A) 3000  B) 4000  C) 3200  D) 3300

**Q11.** Write 47 in Roman numerals.

**Q13.** Find the missing number in the pattern: 5, 10, 15, 20, ___, 30
