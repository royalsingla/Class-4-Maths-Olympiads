# Inserting Missing Numbers — Achievers / HOTS

A harder follow-on to `21-inserting-missing-numbers/`: column-direction patterns, magic-square-style grids (row AND column constraints), two-step rules, number pyramids, and a 2×2 product grid. 15 questions, 4-option MCQ, untimed.

---

**Q1.** This time look DOWN each column, not across each row. Every column adds up to the same total. Find the missing number.

|   |   |   |
|---|---|---|
| 9 | 4 | 7 |
| 6 | 10 | **?** |
| 5 | 6 | 10 |

A) 20  B) 3  C) 6  D) 5
