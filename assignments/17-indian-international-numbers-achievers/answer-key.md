# Indian & International Numbers — Achievers / HOTS — Answer Key

1. **B) 1,520,000** — Fifteen lakh twenty thousand = 15,20,000 = 1520000, regrouped as International: 1,520,000.
2. **A) 23,50,000** — $2,350,000 = 2350000, regrouped as Indian: 23,50,000.
3. **A) 2,100,000** — 12,30,000 + 8,70,000 = 21,00,000 = 2100000 = International 2,100,000.
4. **B) Three million two hundred thousand** — Thirty-two lakh = 32,00,000 = 3200000 = 3,200,000 = "three million two hundred thousand."
5. **D) 8,00,00,000** — In 8,45,67,123, the Crores digit is 8; place value = 8 × 1,00,00,000 = 8,00,00,000.
6. **A) 9 million** — 9 million = 90,00,000; 85 lakh = 85,00,000. 90,00,000 > 85,00,000.
7. **A) 4,125,000** — 18,50,000 + 22,75,000 = 41,25,000 = 4125000 = International 4,125,000.
8. **C) 50** — 5 million = 50,00,000; 50,00,000 ÷ 1,00,000 = 50 lakh.
9. **B) 60** — Six crore = 6,00,00,000 = 60,000,000 = 60 million.
10. **A) 34,520,600** — 3,45,20,600 = 34520600, regrouped as International: 34,520,600.
11. **A) 5,67,89,012** — 56,789,012 = 56789012, regrouped as Indian: 5,67,89,012.
12. **A) 72,500,000** — 7 crore 25 lakh = 7,25,00,000 = 72500000 = International 72,500,000.
13. **A) Twenty-four lakh** — Two million four hundred thousand = 2,400,000 = 24,00,000 = "twenty-four lakh."
14. **B) 4 million** — 25,00,000 + 15,00,000 = 40,00,000 = 4,000,000 = 4 million.
15. **A) 90,00,000 = Nine million** — Indian places from the right: ones, tens, hundreds, thousands, ten-thousands, lakhs, **ten-lakhs** (7th digit). A 9 there with 0s elsewhere gives 90,00,000 = 9,000,000 = "nine million."

---

**How to use this:** 10+/15 is a strong result at this difficulty. Every
question here needs you to convert a number *and* do something with it
(add, compare, or re-express) — if a question tripped you up, redo the
conversion on paper first, then the arithmetic, rather than trying to do
both at once.
