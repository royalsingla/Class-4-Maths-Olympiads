# Indian & International Numbers — Achievers / HOTS

A harder follow-on to `16-indian-international-numbers/`, mixing number-name
conversion with arithmetic and comparison across the two systems — 2-and-3-step
problems rather than direct lookups. 15 questions, 4-option MCQ, untimed.

---

**Q1.** The population of a town is "fifteen lakh twenty thousand." Write this using International-system commas.
A) 1,502,000  B) 1,520,000  C) 1,052,000  D) 152,0000

**Q2.** A company reports its international revenue as $2,350,000. Write the same amount using Indian-system commas.
A) 23,50,000  B) 2,35,0000  C) 235,0000  D) 2,350,000

**Q3.** "Twelve lakh thirty thousand" is added to "eight lakh seventy thousand." Find the total, written with International-system commas.
A) 2,100,000  B) 2,010,000  C) 21,000,00  D) 210,0000

**Q4.** A number is read as "thirty-two lakh" in the Indian system. What is its International-system number name?
A) Thirty-two million  B) Three million two hundred thousand  C) Three million twenty thousand  D) Thirty million two hundred thousand

**Q5.** In the number 8,45,67,123 (Indian system), what is the place value of the digit in the Crores place?
A) 8,000  B) 80,000  C) 8,00,000  D) 8,00,00,000

**Q6.** Which is greater: 9 million, or 85 lakh?
A) 9 million  B) 85 lakh  C) They are equal  D) Cannot be compared

**Q7.** A factory produced "eighteen lakh fifty thousand" toys in one year and "twenty-two lakh seventy-five thousand" the next year. Find the total production, using International-system commas.
A) 4,125,000  B) 4,215,000  C) 41,250,00  D) 4,025,000

**Q8.** How many lakh make 5 million?
A) 5  B) 10  C) 50  D) 500

**Q9.** A number is "six crore" in the Indian system. How many millions is it?
A) 6  B) 60  C) 600  D) 6000

**Q10.** Write 3,45,20,600 (Indian system) using International-system commas.
A) 34,520,600  B) 345,20,600  C) 3,452,0600  D) 34,502,600

**Q11.** The number 56,789,012 is written in the International system. Write the same number using Indian-system commas.
A) 5,67,89,012  B) 56,78,9012  C) 5,678,9012  D) 56,789,012

**Q12.** A number is "7 crore 25 lakh" in the Indian system. Express this value using International-system commas.
A) 72,500,000  B) 7,250,0000  C) 725,00,000  D) 72,050,000

**Q13.** Rani says a number is "two million four hundred thousand." Meena says the same number in the Indian system. What does Meena say?
A) Twenty-four lakh  B) Two lakh forty thousand  C) Twenty-four crore  D) Two crore forty lakh

**Q14.** Find the sum of "twenty-five lakh" and "fifteen lakh," and express the answer in millions.
A) 2 million  B) 4 million  C) 40 million  D) 0.4 million

**Q15.** A 7-digit number has 9 in the Ten Lakhs place and 0 in every other place. What is the number, and what is it called in the International system?
A) 90,00,000 = Nine million  B) 9,00,000 = Nine hundred thousand  C) 90,00,000 = Ninety lakh only  D) 9,00,00,000 = Nine crore
