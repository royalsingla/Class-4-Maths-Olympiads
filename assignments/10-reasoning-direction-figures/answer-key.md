# Week 10 — Reasoning: Ranking, Direction Sense, Days/Dates & Figures — Answer Key

## Part A — Ranking, Direction Sense & Days/Dates

**Q1. A) 12** — Total = (position from left) + (position from right) − 1 = 5 + 8 − 1 = 12.

**Q2. A) 29** — Total = 7 + 23 − 1 = 29.

**Q3. D) Esha** — Order from tallest to shortest: Dev > Chetan > Aman > Bala > Esha.

**Q4. B) 26** — Rank from bottom = total students − rank from top + 1 = 35 − 10 + 1 = 26.

**Q5. A) East** — Facing North and turning 90° clockwise (to the right) faces you East.

**Q6. A) East** — Facing South, a left turn faces you East.

**Q7. A) 6 m East** — 10 m East then 4 m West cancels partly: 10 − 4 = 6 m East of the start.

**Q8. B) 8th** — Adding 7 days keeps the same day of the week: 1 + 7 = 8.

**Q9. B) 17th** — March 1st is Sunday, so March 3rd is the 1st Tuesday, March 10th the 2nd Tuesday, and March 17th the 3rd Tuesday.

**Q10. B) Team B** — Team B > Team A > Team C, and Team D is lowest of all — so Team B is highest.

**Q11. A) East** — The rule shifts each direction one step anticlockwise around the compass (East→North, North→West); following the same turn, South→East.

**Q12. C) Red** — Order from top to bottom: Red, Blue, Green, Yellow.

**Q13. B) Thursday** — 25th=Fri, 26th=Sat, 27th=Sun, 28th=Mon, 29th=Tue, 30th=Wed (last day of the month), so the 1st of next month is Thursday.

**Q14. C) Meera** — Putting it together: Neha > Arjun > Kabir > Meera, so Meera is lowest.

**Q15. A) 14 m North** — Net northward distance = 12 − 5 + 7 = 14 m North.

## Part B — Mirror Images, Embedded Figures & Geometrical Solids

**Q16. B** — A true mirror image flips the figure left-right only (not up-down,
and not a 180° spin). Option B shows F correctly reflected left-right: the
vertical stroke moves to the right side and the arms point left.

**Q17. D** — Option D is L reflected left-right: the vertical stroke moves to
the right side with the foot pointing left at the bottom.

**Q18. A** — Option A is 7 reflected left-right.

> For every mirror question, check each wrong option against what it actually
> is: the original figure unchanged, the figure flipped upside-down
> (top-bottom, not left-right), and the figure spun 180°. All three look
> "different" from the original but are *not* a left-right mirror image —
> only a true left-right flip is.

**Q19. B) Triangle** — The composite figure is a square with a triangle
overlapping its top — the triangle's outline is clearly traceable within it.

**Q20. A) Rectangle** — The composite figure is a circle with a rectangle
overlapping its centre — the rectangle's outline is clearly traceable within it.

**Q21. D) Circle** — The composite figure is a triangle with a circle
overlapping its lower half — the circle's outline is clearly traceable within it.

**Q22. B) 6** — A cube has 6 square faces (even though only 3 are visible in
this drawing, the opposite 3 are hidden behind them).

**Q23. A) Cone** — A cone has one flat circular base and one curved surface
coming to a point (apex).

**Q24. C) 2** — A cylinder has 2 flat circular faces (top and bottom) joined
by one curved surface.
