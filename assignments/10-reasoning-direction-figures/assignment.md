# Week 10 — Reasoning: Ranking, Direction Sense & Days/Dates

15 questions, 4-option MCQ, untimed.

> **Note:** Mirror images, embedded figures, and geometrical-solid recognition
> are diagram-only question types and aren't included here — see the note in
> `assignments/README.md` for how to supplement them.

---

**Q1.** In a line of students, Aman is 5th from the left and 8th from the right. How many students are there in the line?
A) 12  B) 13  C) 11  D) 14

**Q2.** In a class, Priya is ranked 7th from the top and 23rd from the bottom. How many students are there in the class?
A) 29  B) 30  C) 28  D) 31

**Q3.** Five friends are standing in a row. Aman is taller than Bala but shorter than Chetan. Dev is the tallest. Esha is shorter than Bala. Who is the shortest?
A) Aman  B) Bala  C) Chetan  D) Esha

**Q4.** Ramesh ranks 10th from the top in a class of 35 students. What is his rank from the bottom?
A) 25  B) 26  C) 24  D) 27

**Q5.** Facing North, Arjun turns 90° clockwise. Which direction is he facing now?
A) East  B) West  C) South  D) North

**Q6.** A man walks 4 km South, then turns left and walks 3 km. In which direction is he walking now?
A) East  B) West  C) North  D) South

**Q7.** Sonia walks 10 m East, then 4 m West. How far, and in which direction, is she from her starting point now?
A) 6 m East  B) 14 m East  C) 6 m West  D) 4 m East

**Q8.** If Monday falls on 1st of a month, what date will the next Monday fall on?
A) 7th  B) 8th  C) 9th  D) 14th

**Q9.** Sarita's birthday is on the 3rd Tuesday of March. If March 1st is a Sunday, on which date is her birthday?
A) 10th  B) 17th  C) 24th  D) 3rd

**Q10.** In a game, Team A scored more than Team C but less than Team B. Team D scored the least of all. Which team scored the highest?
A) Team A  B) Team B  C) Team C  D) Team D

**Q11.** If East becomes North, and North becomes West (following a fixed rule), what does South become?
A) East  B) West  C) North  D) South

**Q12.** Four boxes are stacked. The red box is above the blue box. The green box is below the blue box. The yellow box is at the very bottom, below the green box. Which box is on top?
A) Blue  B) Green  C) Red  D) Yellow

**Q13.** Today is the 25th of a month and it is a Friday. If this month has 30 days, what day of the week will the 1st of next month be?
A) Wednesday  B) Thursday  C) Friday  D) Saturday

**Q14.** Neha scored more than Kabir. Kabir scored more than Meera but less than Arjun. Arjun scored less than Neha. Who scored the lowest?
A) Neha  B) Kabir  C) Meera  D) Arjun

**Q15.** Karan walks 12 m North, then 5 m South, then 7 m North. How far, and in which direction, is he from his starting point now?
A) 14 m North  B) 10 m North  C) 24 m North  D) 14 m South
