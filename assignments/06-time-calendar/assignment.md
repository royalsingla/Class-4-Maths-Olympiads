# Week 6 — Time & Calendar

15 questions, 4-option MCQ, untimed.

---

**Q1.** How many minutes make 1 hour?
A) 30  B) 45  C) 60  D) 100

**Q2.** How many hours make 1 day?
A) 12  B) 24  C) 7  D) 48

**Q3.** How many days are there in the month of April?
A) 28  B) 29  C) 30  D) 31

**Q4.** How many months in a year have 31 days?
A) 5  B) 6  C) 7  D) 8

**Q5.** A movie starts at 4:30 p.m. and lasts for 2 hours 15 minutes. At what time will it end?
A) 6:30 p.m.  B) 6:45 p.m.  C) 7:00 p.m.  D) 6:15 p.m.

**Q6.** A train leaves the station at 9:45 a.m. and reaches its destination at 1:20 p.m. How long is the journey?
A) 3 h 25 min  B) 3 h 35 min  C) 4 h 5 min  D) 3 h 45 min

**Q7.** What is the day after Friday?
A) Thursday  B) Saturday  C) Sunday  D) Monday

**Q8.** If 1st January is a Monday, what day will 8th January be?
A) Sunday  B) Monday  C) Tuesday  D) Wednesday

**Q9.** How many weeks are there in 28 days?
A) 3  B) 4  C) 5  D) 7

**Q10.** How many seconds make 1 minute?
A) 30  B) 60  C) 100  D) 120

**Q11.** Riya's birthday is on 15th August. If today is 10th August, how many days are left for her birthday?
A) 4  B) 5  C) 6  D) 10

**Q12.** A clock shows 10:50. What time will it show after 25 minutes?
A) 11:05  B) 11:10  C) 11:15  D) 11:20

**Q13.** Which of these years is a leap year?
A) 2021  B) 2022  C) 2023  D) 2024

**Q14.** A school assembly starts at 7:50 a.m. and the first period begins at 8:30 a.m. How long is the assembly?
A) 30 min  B) 35 min  C) 40 min  D) 45 min

**Q15.** Rohit's train journey began at 11:40 p.m. on Monday and ended 5 hours later. On which day and at what time did it end?
A) Monday, 4:40 a.m.  B) Tuesday, 4:40 a.m.  C) Tuesday, 5:40 a.m.  D) Monday, 5:40 a.m.
