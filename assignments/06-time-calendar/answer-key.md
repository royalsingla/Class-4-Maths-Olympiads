# Week 6 — Time & Calendar — Answer Key

**Q1. C) 60** — 1 hour = 60 minutes.

**Q2. B) 24** — 1 day = 24 hours.

**Q3. C) 30** — April has 30 days.

**Q4. C) 7** — January, March, May, July, August, October, December each have 31 days.

**Q5. B) 6:45 p.m.** — 4:30 p.m. + 2 h = 6:30 p.m.; + 15 min more = 6:45 p.m.

**Q6. B) 3 h 35 min** — 9:45 a.m. to 12:45 p.m. is 3 hours; 12:45 p.m. to 1:20 p.m. is 35 more minutes; total 3 h 35 min.

**Q7. B) Saturday** — The day right after Friday is Saturday.

**Q8. B) Monday** — 8 days later than the 1st is the 8th; since a week has 7 days, the day of the week repeats after 7 days, so the 8th is also a Monday.

**Q9. B) 4** — 28 ÷ 7 = 4 weeks.

**Q10. B) 60** — 1 minute = 60 seconds.

**Q11. B) 5** — 15 − 10 = 5 days.

**Q12. C) 11:15** — 10:50 + 25 min = 11:15.

**Q13. D) 2024** — A year is a leap year if it divides exactly by 4 (and, for century years, by 400 too). 2024 ÷ 4 = 506 exactly.

**Q14. C) 40 min** — 7:50 a.m. to 8:30 a.m. is 40 minutes.

**Q15. B) Tuesday, 4:40 a.m.** — 11:40 p.m. + 5 hours crosses midnight: 11:40 p.m. → 12:40 a.m. (1 h) → ... → 4:40 a.m. (5 h total), and since it crossed midnight, the day becomes Tuesday.
