# Inserting Missing Numbers

A dedicated worksheet for the **Inserting Missing Numbers** reasoning puzzle type — identified as a gap against the school's own syllabus. Each puzzle shows a grid (or a small pyramid) of numbers following a hidden rule; work out the rule from the complete rows, then find the missing number. 15 questions, 4-option MCQ, untimed.

---
