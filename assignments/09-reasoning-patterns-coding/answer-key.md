# Week 9 — Reasoning: Patterns, Analogy, Classification & Coding-Decoding — Answer Key

**Q1. D) Chair** — Dog, Cat, and Cow are animals; a Chair is furniture.

**Q2. B) Vegetable** — Apple belongs to the group "Fruit"; Carrot belongs to the group "Vegetable."

**Q3. C) 7** — 2, 4, and 8 are even numbers; 7 is odd.

**Q4. A) Sing** — You read a book; in the same way, you sing a song.

**Q5. A) ECV** — Each letter moves forward 2 steps: C→E, A→C, T→V, giving ECV.

**Q6. A) BQQMF** — Each letter moves forward 1 step: A→B, P→Q, P→Q, L→M, E→F, giving BQQMF.

**Q7. B) I** — The pattern skips one letter each time: A, C, E, G, I.

**Q8. A) R** — The pattern skips one letter backward each time: Z, X, V, T, R.

**Q9. C) Cube** — Circle, Oval, and Square are flat (2-D) shapes; a Cube is a solid (3-D) shape.

**Q10. D) Mango** — Rose, Lily, and Lotus are flowers; Mango is a fruit.

**Q11. C) 20** — 9, 16, and 25 are perfect squares (3², 4², 5²); 20 is not.

**Q12. A) 16-5-14** — P is the 16th letter, E is the 5th, N is the 14th.

**Q13. B) 10** — The pattern adds 2 each time: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12.

**Q14. A) DIBJS** — Each letter moves forward 1 step: C→D, H→I, A→B, I→J, R→S, giving DIBJS.

**Q15. D) 23** — 1+2=3, 2+1=3, 3+0=3, but 2+3=5, so 23 is the odd one out.
