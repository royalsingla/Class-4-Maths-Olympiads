# Indian & International Numbers — Level 2

A dedicated harder worksheet for students who've qualified Level 1 and are
prepping for Level 2 — bigger numbers (into crores/hundred-millions),
multi-step conversions, and conversions combined with arithmetic. 15
questions, 4-option MCQ, untimed.

---

**Q1.** Write 7,08,45,219 (Indian system) using International-system commas.
A) 70,845,219  B) 7,084,5219  C) 708,45,219  D) 70,854,219

**Q2.** Write 123,456,789 (International system) using Indian-system commas.
A) 12,34,56,789  B) 1,23,45,6789  C) 123,45,6,789  D) 12,34,65,789

**Q3.** "1 crore 25 lakh" is added to "2 crore 75 lakh." Express the sum using International-system commas.
A) 40,000,000  B) 4,000,000  C) 400,00,000  D) 40,500,000

**Q4.** How many crore make 500 million?
A) 5  B) 50  C) 500  D) 5000

**Q5.** A country's exports were "forty-five crore" in the Indian system. Express this in millions (International system).
A) 45 million  B) 450 million  C) 4,500 million  D) 4.5 million

**Q6.** 2,34,56,789 (Indian system) minus 1,00,00,000 equals — write the answer with Indian-system commas.
A) 1,34,56,789  B) 1,24,56,789  C) 13,45,6,789  D) 1,34,65,789

**Q7.** "Three crore" is how many times "sixty lakh"?
A) 2  B) 5  C) 6  D) 50

**Q8.** A library has "one crore twenty lakh" books. Express this as a number in millions (International system).
A) 1.2 million  B) 12 million  C) 120 million  D) 1,200 million

**Q9.** Find the sum of 15 million and 25 million, and express the answer in crore (Indian system).
A) 4 crore  B) 40 crore  C) 0.4 crore  D) 400 crore

**Q10.** A number has 3 in the Crores place and 0 in every other place (Indian system). Write the number with Indian-system commas.
A) 30,00,000  B) 3,00,00,000  C) 3,00,000  D) 30,00,00,000

**Q11.** Convert 3,00,00,000 (Indian system) into its International-system number name.
A) Three hundred million  B) Thirty million  C) Three million  D) Three hundred thousand

**Q12.** A factory's annual output is "2 crore 40 lakh" units. If production is split equally across 12 months, how many units per month? (Indian-system commas)
A) 2,00,000  B) 20,00,000  C) 2,00,00,000  D) 20,00,0000

**Q13.** Which is greater: 7 crore, or 65 million?
A) 7 crore  B) 65 million  C) They are equal  D) Cannot be compared

**Q14.** Find the difference between 1 crore and 85 lakh, and express the answer in thousands.
A) 15  B) 150  C) 1,500  D) 15,000

**Q15.** A number reads "five crore six lakh" in the Indian system. Write its International-system number name.
A) Fifty million six hundred thousand  B) Five million six hundred thousand  C) Fifty-six million  D) Five hundred six million
