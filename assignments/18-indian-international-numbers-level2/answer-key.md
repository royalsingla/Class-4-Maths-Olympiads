# Indian & International Numbers — Level 2 — Answer Key

1. **A) 70,845,219** — 7,08,45,219 = 70845219, regrouped as International: 70,845,219.
2. **A) 12,34,56,789** — 123,456,789 = 123456789, regrouped as Indian: 12,34,56,789.
3. **A) 40,000,000** — 1,25,00,000 + 2,75,00,000 = 4,00,00,000 = 40000000 = International 40,000,000.
4. **B) 50** — 500 million = 500,000,000; 500,000,000 ÷ 10,000,000 (1 crore) = 50.
5. **B) 450 million** — Forty-five crore = 45,00,00,000 = 450,000,000 = 450 million.
6. **A) 1,34,56,789** — 2,34,56,789 − 1,00,00,000 = 1,34,56,789 (23456789 − 10000000 = 13456789).
7. **B) 5** — Three crore = 3,00,00,000; sixty lakh = 60,00,000. 3,00,00,000 ÷ 60,00,000 = 5.
8. **B) 12 million** — One crore twenty lakh = 1,20,00,000 = 12,000,000 = 12 million.
9. **A) 4 crore** — 15,000,000 + 25,000,000 = 40,000,000 = 4,00,00,000 = 4 crore.
10. **B) 3,00,00,000** — A 3 in the Crores place (the 8th digit from the right in the Indian system) with 0s elsewhere gives 3,00,00,000.
11. **B) Thirty million** — 3,00,00,000 = 30000000 = 30,000,000 = "thirty million."
12. **B) 20,00,000** — 2,40,00,000 ÷ 12 = 20,00,000 (24000000 ÷ 12 = 2000000).
13. **A) 7 crore** — 7 crore = 7,00,00,000 = 70,000,000; 65 million = 65,000,000. 70,000,000 > 65,000,000.
14. **C) 1,500** — 1,00,00,000 − 85,00,000 = 15,00,000 = 1,500,000; in thousands, 1,500,000 ÷ 1,000 = 1,500.
15. **A) Fifty million six hundred thousand** — Five crore six lakh = 5,06,00,000 = 50600000 = 50,600,000 = "fifty million six hundred thousand."

---

**How to use this:** 10+/15 is strong at this difficulty. The numbers
here go well past what Level 1 uses (into crores and hundred-millions) —
if the scale trips you up, write out the full place value chart for the
number first (digit by digit, right to left) before trying to regroup or
calculate with it.
