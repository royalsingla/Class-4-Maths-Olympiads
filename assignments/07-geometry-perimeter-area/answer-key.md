# Week 7 — Geometry, Perimeter & Area — Answer Key

**Q1. B) 6** — A hexagon has 6 sides.

**Q2. C) Circle** — A circle is a curved shape with no corners.

**Q3. D) 4** — A square has 4 lines of symmetry (2 diagonal, 1 vertical, 1 horizontal).

**Q4. B) A** — The letter A has a vertical line of symmetry; F, P, and N do not look the same when folded in half.

**Q5. C) Line** — A line extends forever in both directions, with no endpoints.

**Q6. C) Line segment** — A line segment has exactly two endpoints.

**Q7. A) 36 cm** — Perimeter of a square = 4 × side = 4 × 9 = 36 cm.

**Q8. B) 18 cm** — Perimeter of a triangle = sum of all sides = 5 + 6 + 7 = 18 cm.

**Q9. A) 6 cm** — Perimeter = 2 × (length + breadth), so 28 = 2 × (8 + breadth) → 14 = 8 + breadth → breadth = 6 cm.

**Q10. B) 24 sq cm** — Area of a rectangle = length × breadth = 6 × 4 = 24 sq cm.

**Q11. C) 25 sq cm** — Area of a square = side × side = 5 × 5 = 25 sq cm.

**Q12. C) 4** — A complete turn is 360°, and each right angle is 90°; 360 ÷ 90 = 4.

**Q13. C) Sphere** — A sphere (like a ball) is entirely curved with no flat faces at all.

**Q14. B) 6** — A cube has 6 square faces.

**Q15. B) 50 m** — Perimeter = 2 × (15 + 10) = 2 × 25 = 50 m.
