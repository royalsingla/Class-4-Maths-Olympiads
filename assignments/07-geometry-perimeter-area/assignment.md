# Week 7 — Geometry, Perimeter & Area

Shapes, lines, symmetry, plus perimeter and basic area. 15 questions,
4-option MCQ, untimed.

---

**Q1.** How many sides does a hexagon have?
A) 5  B) 6  C) 7  D) 8

**Q2.** Which shape has no corners?
A) Square  B) Triangle  C) Circle  D) Rectangle

**Q3.** How many lines of symmetry does a square have?
A) 1  B) 2  C) 3  D) 4

**Q4.** Which of these capital letters has a line of symmetry?
A) F  B) A  C) P  D) N

**Q5.** A line that goes on forever in both directions is called a:
A) Line segment  B) Ray  C) Line  D) Point

**Q6.** A part of a line with two endpoints is called a:
A) Ray  B) Line  C) Line segment  D) Angle

**Q7.** Find the perimeter of a square with side 9 cm.
A) 36 cm  B) 18 cm  C) 81 cm  D) 27 cm

**Q8.** Find the perimeter of a triangle with sides 5 cm, 6 cm, and 7 cm.
A) 16 cm  B) 18 cm  C) 20 cm  D) 17 cm

**Q9.** A rectangle has a perimeter of 28 cm. If its length is 8 cm, what is its breadth?
A) 6 cm  B) 8 cm  C) 10 cm  D) 4 cm

**Q10.** Find the area of a rectangle with length 6 cm and breadth 4 cm.
A) 20 sq cm  B) 24 sq cm  C) 10 sq cm  D) 28 sq cm

**Q11.** Find the area of a square with side 5 cm.
A) 20 sq cm  B) 10 sq cm  C) 25 sq cm  D) 30 sq cm

**Q12.** How many right angles make a complete turn?
A) 2  B) 3  C) 4  D) 6

**Q13.** Which of these solids has a curved surface and no flat face at all?
A) Cube  B) Cone  C) Sphere  D) Cylinder

**Q14.** How many faces does a cube have?
A) 4  B) 6  C) 8  D) 12

**Q15.** A rectangular garden is 15 m long and 10 m wide. Find the perimeter of the garden.
A) 25 m  B) 50 m  C) 150 m  D) 100 m
