# Achievers — Level 2 (Multi-Step HOTS)

A dedicated harder follow-on to Week 11's Achievers set, for students who've
qualified Level 1 and are prepping for Level 2. Every question needs two or
more genuine reasoning steps — working backwards, combining two facts, or
solving for an unknown before using it. 15 questions, 4-option MCQ, untimed.
Give yourself real thinking time; this is meant to be harder than anything
in the core 12-week plan.

---

**Q1.** A number is first divided by 4, then 15 is added to the result, giving 27. Find the original number.
A) 44  B) 46  C) 48  D) 50

**Q2.** The sum of three numbers is 170. The second number is 3 times the first, and the third is 5 less than the second. Find the third number.
A) 65  B) 68  C) 70  D) 72

**Q3.** Rohan had some money. He spent 3/7 of it on toys, then 1/4 of what remained on books, and was left with ₹720. How much money did he have at first?
A) ₹1,440  B) ₹1,560  C) ₹1,680  D) ₹1,800

**Q4.** A father's age is 4 times his son's age. After 5 years, the father will be 3 times the son's age. Find the son's current age.
A) 8  B) 9  C) 10  D) 12

**Q5.** Pipe A can fill a tank in 6 hours. Pipe B can fill the same tank in 12 hours. If both pipes are opened together, how long will they take to fill the tank?
A) 3 hours  B) 4 hours  C) 5 hours  D) 8 hours

**Q6.** The sum of three consecutive even numbers is 90. Find the largest of the three numbers.
A) 28  B) 30  C) 32  D) 34

**Q7.** The sum of two numbers is 86 and their difference is 14. Find the larger number.
A) 46  B) 48  C) 50  D) 52

**Q8.** Ravi has only ₹5 and ₹2 coins. He has 18 coins in total, worth ₹69 altogether. How many ₹5 coins does he have?
A) 9  B) 10  C) 11  D) 12

**Q9.** A train travels 180 km in 3 hours at a steady speed. At the same speed, how long will it take to travel 420 km?
A) 6 hours  B) 7 hours  C) 8 hours  D) 9 hours

**Q10.** A two-digit number is 4 times the sum of its digits. The ones digit is 3 more than the tens digit. Find the number.
A) 24  B) 30  C) 36  D) 48

**Q11.** The length of a rectangle is 5 cm more than its breadth. If the perimeter is 54 cm, find the area of the rectangle.
A) 154 sq cm  B) 165 sq cm  C) 176 sq cm  D) 187 sq cm

**Q12.** A sequence is formed by adding the two numbers just before a term and then subtracting 1. It starts 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, ___. Find the 6th term.
A) 12  B) 13  C) 14  D) 15

**Q13.** A shopkeeper had some pens. He sold 1/3 of them on Monday and 24 more on Tuesday. If 56 pens are left, how many pens did he have originally?
A) 100  B) 110  C) 120  D) 130

**Q14.** Three bells ring at intervals of 12 minutes, 15 minutes, and 20 minutes. If all three ring together at 9:00 a.m., at what time will they next ring together?
A) 9:40 a.m.  B) 9:50 a.m.  C) 10:00 a.m.  D) 10:10 a.m.

**Q15.** A box contains some sweets. If divided equally among 8 children, 3 sweets are left over. If divided equally among 9 children, 2 sweets are left over. The box has more than 50 but fewer than 100 sweets. How many sweets are in the box?
A) 75  B) 79  C) 83  D) 91
