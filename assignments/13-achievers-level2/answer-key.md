# Achievers — Level 2 — Answer Key

1. **C) 48** — Working backwards: (27 − 15) × 4 = 12 × 4 = 48.
2. **C) 70** — Let the first number be *x*. Then second = 3*x*, third = 3*x*−5. Sum: *x* + 3*x* + (3*x*−5) = 170 → 7*x* = 175 → *x* = 25. Third = 3×25−5 = 70.
3. **C) ₹1,680** — After spending 3/7 on toys, 4/7 of the money remains. After spending 1/4 of that on books, 3/4 of 4/7 = 3/7 of the original remains. So 3/7 of the total = ₹720 → total = ₹1,680.
4. **C) 10** — Let the son's age be *x*; father's age = 4*x*. After 5 years: 4*x*+5 = 3(*x*+5) → 4*x*+5 = 3*x*+15 → *x* = 10.
5. **B) 4 hours** — Pipe A fills 1/6 of the tank per hour, Pipe B fills 1/12 per hour. Together: 1/6 + 1/12 = 3/12 = 1/4 of the tank per hour, so the tank fills in 4 hours.
6. **C) 32** — Let the numbers be *x*, *x*+2, *x*+4. Sum: 3*x*+6 = 90 → *x* = 28. Largest = 28+4 = 32.
7. **C) 50** — Larger number = (sum + difference) ÷ 2 = (86+14) ÷ 2 = 50.
8. **C) 11** — Let *a* = number of ₹5 coins, *b* = number of ₹2 coins. *a*+*b* = 18 and 5*a*+2*b* = 69. Substituting *b* = 18−*a*: 5*a* + 2(18−*a*) = 69 → 3*a* + 36 = 69 → *a* = 11.
9. **B) 7 hours** — Speed = 180 ÷ 3 = 60 km/h. Time for 420 km = 420 ÷ 60 = 7 hours.
10. **C) 36** — Let the tens digit be *t*; the ones digit is *t*+3. The number = 11*t*+3, and the digit sum = 2*t*+3. Setting 11*t*+3 = 4(2*t*+3) gives 11*t*+3 = 8*t*+12 → *t* = 3. Number = 11×3+3 = 36. Check: digit sum = 3+6 = 9, and 4×9 = 36. ✓
11. **C) 176 sq cm** — Perimeter = 2(*l*+*b*) = 54 → *l*+*b* = 27. Since *l* = *b*+5: 2*b*+5 = 27 → *b* = 11, *l* = 16. Area = 11×16 = 176 sq cm.
12. **C) 14** — Each term = (sum of previous two) − 1: after 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, the next term is 6+9−1 = 14.
13. **C) 120** — Let the original number of pens be *x*. After Monday, 2/3 of *x* remain. After Tuesday: 2/3*x* − 24 = 56 → 2/3*x* = 80 → *x* = 120.
14. **C) 10:00 a.m.** — The bells next ring together after the LCM of 12, 15, and 20 minutes, which is 60 minutes. 9:00 a.m. + 60 min = 10:00 a.m.
15. **C) 83** — The number must leave remainder 3 when divided by 8 and remainder 2 when divided by 9. Checking numbers between 50 and 100 that fit both conditions gives 83 (83 ÷ 8 = 10 remainder 3; 83 ÷ 9 = 9 remainder 2).
