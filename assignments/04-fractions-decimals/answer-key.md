# Week 4 — Fractions & Decimals — Answer Key

**Q1. A) 3/8** — 3 shaded parts out of 8 equal parts.

**Q2. B) 3/6** — 3/6 simplifies to 1/2 (divide top and bottom by 3).

**Q3. B) 2/5 < 3/5** — Same denominator, so compare numerators: 2 < 3.

**Q4. D) 1/5** — When the numerator is the same (1), a bigger denominator means smaller pieces, so 1/5 is the smallest.

**Q5. A) 2/3** — The greatest common factor of 8 and 12 is 4; 8÷4 = 2, 12÷4 = 3.

**Q6. A) 3/4** — Same denominator: add the numerators only. 1/4 + 2/4 = 3/4.

**Q7. A) 1/2** — 5/6 − 2/6 = 3/6, which simplifies to 1/2.

**Q8. A) 1/2** — 0.5 = 5/10 = 1/2.

**Q9. A) 0.3** — 3/10 means 3 tenths, written as 0.3.

**Q10. B) 0.5** — 0.50 is greater than 0.45.

**Q11. A) 0.7** — 7/10 means 7 tenths, written as 0.7.

**Q12. A) 0.7** — 0.3 + 0.4 = 0.7.

**Q13. A) 5/8** — Same denominator: 2/8 + 3/8 = 5/8.

**Q14. C) 0.6 m** — 1 − 0.4 = 0.6.

**Q15. D) 1/2** — As decimals: 1/3 ≈ 0.33, 2/5 = 0.4, 3/8 = 0.375, 1/2 = 0.5 — the largest is 1/2.
