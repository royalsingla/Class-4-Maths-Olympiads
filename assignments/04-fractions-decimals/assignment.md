# Week 4 — Fractions & Decimals

15 questions, 4-option MCQ, untimed.

---

**Q1.** A figure is divided into 8 equal parts, and 3 of them are shaded. What fraction is shaded?
A) 3/8  B) 8/3  C) 5/8  D) 3/5

**Q2.** Which fraction is equivalent to 1/2?
A) 2/5  B) 3/6  C) 2/3  D) 4/5

**Q3.** Compare: 2/5 ___ 3/5
A) 2/5 > 3/5  B) 2/5 < 3/5  C) 2/5 = 3/5  D) Cannot compare

**Q4.** Which is the smallest fraction?
A) 1/2  B) 1/4  C) 1/3  D) 1/5

**Q5.** Reduce 8/12 to its simplest form.
A) 2/3  B) 4/6  C) 1/2  D) 3/4

**Q6.** 1/4 + 2/4 = ?
A) 3/4  B) 3/8  C) 1/2  D) 2/4

**Q7.** 5/6 − 2/6 = ?
A) 1/2  B) 3/12  C) 7/6  D) 1/3

**Q8.** What is 0.5 written as a fraction?
A) 1/2  B) 1/5  C) 5/1  D) 2/5

**Q9.** What is 3/10 written as a decimal?
A) 0.3  B) 0.03  C) 3.0  D) 0.13

**Q10.** Which decimal is greater: 0.45 or 0.5?
A) 0.45  B) 0.5  C) Both equal  D) Cannot say

**Q11.** Convert 7/10 to a decimal.
A) 0.7  B) 0.07  C) 7.1  D) 1.7

**Q12.** 0.3 + 0.4 = ?
A) 0.7  B) 0.07  C) 7  D) 0.34

**Q13.** Priya ate 2/8 of a pizza and her brother ate 3/8 of the same pizza. What fraction of the pizza did they eat together?
A) 5/8  B) 5/16  C) 1/8  D) 6/8

**Q14.** A ribbon is 1 metre long. If 0.4 m is cut off, how much ribbon is left?
A) 0.4 m  B) 0.5 m  C) 0.6 m  D) 0.7 m

**Q15.** Which of these fractions is the greatest: 1/3, 2/5, 3/8, 1/2?
A) 1/3  B) 2/5  C) 3/8  D) 1/2
