# Indian & International Number Systems

The same number is grouped into periods differently depending on the
system. **Indian system** (from the right): Ones, Tens, Hundreds,
Thousands, Ten Thousands, **Lakhs**, Ten Lakhs, **Crores**... — the first
comma comes after 3 digits, then every 2 digits. **International
system**: Ones, Tens, Hundreds, Thousands, Ten Thousands, Hundred
Thousands, **Millions**... — a comma every 3 digits throughout.
15 questions, 4-option MCQ, untimed.
