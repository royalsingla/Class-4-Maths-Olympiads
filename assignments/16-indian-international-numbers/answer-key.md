# Indian & International Number Systems — Answer Key

1. **A) Lakhs** — Indian periods from the right: Ones, Thousands, Lakhs, Crores...
2. **B) Millions** — International periods from the right: Ones, Thousands, Millions...
3. **C) 7** — 7,45,239: reading right to left, the digits are 9 (ones), 3 (tens), 2 (hundreds), 5 (thousands), 4 (ten thousands), 7 (lakhs).
4. **C) 7** — 745,239: reading right to left, the digits are 9 (ones), 3 (tens), 2 (hundreds), 5 (thousands), 4 (ten thousands), 7 (hundred thousands). This is the *same* number as Q3 — only the comma placement and period names differ between the two systems.
5. **A) 2,345,678** — Same number (23,45,678 = 2345678), regrouped every 3 digits from the right.
6. **A) 23,45,678** — Same number (2,345,678 = 2345678), regrouped as 3 digits then every 2 digits from the right.
7. **B) 1,00,000** — 1 lakh = 100,000.
8. **C) 10 lakh** — 1 million = 1,000,000 = 10 × 1,00,000.
9. **B) 10 million** — 1 crore = 1,00,00,000 = 10,000,000 = 10 × 1,000,000.
10. **A) 12,34,506** — Twelve lakh (12,00,000) + thirty-four thousand (34,000) + five hundred six (506) = 12,34,506.
11. **B) 1,200,000** — One million (1,000,000) + two hundred thousand (200,000) = 1,200,000.
12. **B) 10** — 1,00,000 ÷ 10,000 = 10.
13. **C) 1000** — 1,000,000 ÷ 1,000 = 1000.
14. **A) Forty-five lakh sixty-seven thousand eight hundred ninety** — 45,67,890: 45 lakh + 67 thousand + 890.
15. **B) Four million five hundred sixty-seven thousand eight hundred ninety** — 4,567,890: 4 million + 567 thousand + 890.

---

**How to use this:** 12+/15 means you've got the core idea — the two
systems are just different ways of grouping the *same* number with
commas, not different numbers. If you're below that, re-draw both place
value charts side by side (Indian: ...Lakhs, Ten Lakhs, Crores vs.
International: ...Hundred Thousands, Millions) and practise converting
a handful of numbers back and forth before moving to the Achievers set.
