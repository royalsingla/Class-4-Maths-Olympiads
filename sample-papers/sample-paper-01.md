# Sample Paper 1 — Class 4 Mathematics Olympiad

**Pattern:** SOF IMO Level 1 — 35 questions, 40 marks, 60 minutes, no
negative marking. Set a timer before you start (or do it untimed the first
time, per Week 11 of the practice plan).

---

## Section 1 — Logical Reasoning (Q1–Q10, 1 mark each)

**Q1.** Find the odd one out:
A) Circle  B) Square  C) Triangle  D) Sphere

**Q2.** Triangle is to 3 sides as Pentagon is to ___ sides.
A) 4  B) 5  C) 6  D) 7

**Q3.** If each letter of a word is moved one step forward in the alphabet
(A→B, B→C, …), CAT becomes DBU. Using the same rule, what does DOG become?
A) EPH  B) EPG  C) DPH  D) EOH

**Q4.** In a race, Rohan finished before Simmi but after Tanvi. Who
finished first?
A) Rohan  B) Simmi  C) Tanvi  D) Cannot be determined

**Q5.** Priya walks 5 m North, then 3 m East, then 5 m South. In which
direction is she now from her starting point?
A) North  B) South  C) East  D) West

**Q6.** Look at the series: 2, 6, 18, 54, ___. What number comes next?
A) 108  B) 150  C) 162  D) 216

**Q7.** If today is Wednesday, what day will it be after 10 days?
A) Friday  B) Saturday  C) Sunday  D) Monday

**Q8.** In the English alphabet, which letter is exactly the 7th letter
(counting A as 1st)?
A) F  B) G  C) H  D) I

**Q9.** Three of the following numbers are alike in a way, and one is
different. Find the odd one.
A) 16  B) 25  C) 36  D) 42

**Q10.** Study the pattern:
Row 1: 3 + 5 = 8
Row 2: 4 + 6 = 10
Row 3: 5 + 7 = ?
A) 11  B) 12  C) 13  D) 14

## Section 2 — Mathematical Reasoning (Q11–Q20, 1 mark each)

**Q11.** 3,456 + 2,789 = ?
A) 6245  B) 6235  C) 6145  D) 6345

**Q12.** 8,002 − 3,456 = ?
A) 4546  B) 4556  C) 4646  D) 4536

**Q13.** 234 × 6 = ?
A) 1404  B) 1434  C) 1364  D) 1444

**Q14.** 936 ÷ 8 = ?
A) 107  B) 117  C) 127  D) 113

**Q15.** Which fraction is equivalent to 2/3?
A) 4/6  B) 3/5  C) 5/6  D) 2/5

**Q16.** Which is greater: 3/4 or 2/3?
A) 3/4  B) 2/3  C) Both equal  D) Cannot say

**Q17.** Convert 3 km 250 m into metres.
A) 3025 m  B) 3250 m  C) 3520 m  D) 3205 m

**Q18.** 4 kg 500 g + 2 kg 750 g = ?
A) 6 kg 250 g  B) 7 kg 250 g  C) 7 kg 750 g  D) 6 kg 750 g

**Q19.** How many right angles are there in a square?
A) 2  B) 3  C) 4  D) 8

**Q20.** Find the perimeter of a rectangle with length 12 cm and breadth 7 cm.
A) 19 cm  B) 38 cm  C) 76 cm  D) 84 cm

## Section 3 — Everyday Mathematics (Q21–Q30, 1 mark each)

**Q21.** Rani had ₹500. She bought a book for ₹125 and a pen for ₹35. How
much money is left with her?
A) ₹340  B) ₹360  C) ₹330  D) ₹350

**Q22.** A school bus carries 45 students in each trip. How many students
can it carry in 6 trips?
A) 260  B) 270  C) 280  D) 290

**Q23.** A shopkeeper has 456 apples. He packs them into boxes of 8 apples
each. How many boxes does he need, and how many apples are left over?
A) 56 boxes, 8 left  B) 57 boxes, 0 left  C) 57 boxes, 8 left  D) 58 boxes, 0 left

**Q24.** Reema's school starts at 8:15 a.m. and ends at 2:45 p.m. How long
is the school day?
A) 6 h 15 min  B) 6 h 30 min  C) 6 h 45 min  D) 7 h

**Q25.** A recipe needs 250 mL of milk for 1 person. How much milk is
needed for 5 persons?
A) 1 L  B) 1 L 250 mL  C) 1 L 500 mL  D) 1250 L

**Q26.** Aman buys 3 notebooks at ₹45 each and 2 pencils at ₹8 each. What
is the total amount he spends?
A) ₹141  B) ₹151  C) ₹161  D) ₹145

**Q27.** A tank holds 120 litres of water. If 1/4 of the water is used, how
much water is left?
A) 30 L  B) 60 L  C) 90 L  D) 100 L

**Q28.** Ravi's weight is 32 kg and his sister's weight is 27 kg. What is
their total weight?
A) 59 kg  B) 49 kg  C) 69 kg  D) 55 kg

**Q29.** A farmer plants trees in rows. Each row has 15 trees, and there
are 12 rows. How many trees are there in total?
A) 170  B) 180  C) 190  D) 160

**Q30.** The temperature at 6 a.m. was 18°C. By noon it rose by 9°C. What
was the temperature at noon?
A) 25°C  B) 27°C  C) 29°C  D) 23°C

## Section 4 — Achievers Section (Q31–Q35, 2 marks each)

**Q31.** A number, when divided by 7, gives a quotient of 23 and a
remainder of 4. What is the number?
A) 161  B) 165  C) 169  D) 172

**Q32.** The sum of two numbers is 1,250. One of the numbers is 475. Find
the difference between the two numbers.
A) 300  B) 250  C) 350  D) 400

**Q33.** Priya has ₹1,000. She spends half of it on clothes. From the
money remaining, she spends 1/4 on books. How much money does she have
left now?
A) ₹250  B) ₹300  C) ₹375  D) ₹400

**Q34.** Aditi has twice as many marbles as Bhanu. Chirag has 5 more
marbles than Aditi. If Bhanu has 12 marbles, how many marbles do all three
have together?
A) 55  B) 60  C) 65  D) 70

**Q35.** A pattern of joined squares is made with matchsticks: 1 square
uses 4 matchsticks, 2 joined squares use 7, 3 joined squares use 10.
Following this pattern, how many matchsticks are needed for 6 joined
squares?
A) 16  B) 19  C) 21  D) 22
