# Sample Paper 9 — Class 4 Mathematics Olympiad (Level‑2 Difficulty Step-Up)

**Pattern:** Same 35 questions / 40 marks / 60 minutes / no negative marking
as Papers 1–8, but noticeably harder throughout — closer to SOF IMO
**Level 2**.

---

## Section 1 — Logical Reasoning (Q1–Q10, 1 mark each)

**Q1.** Find the odd one out:
A) Rose  B) Lily  C) Jasmine  D) Cabbage

**Q2.** Fish is to Water as Bird is to ___.
A) Sky  B) Nest  C) Tree  D) Wing

**Q3.** If each letter of a word is moved 4 steps forward in the alphabet (wrapping Z→D), CAT becomes GEX. Using the same rule, what does SOLVE become?
A) WSPZI  B) WRPZI  C) WSPZJ  D) XSPZI

**Q4.** This series follows two patterns at once — the 1st, 3rd, 5th, 7th terms, and the 2nd, 4th, 6th, 8th terms. Find the missing (8th) term: 4, 60, 8, 55, 12, 50, 16, ___
A) 43  B) 44  C) 45  D) 46

**Q5.** Five friends — Farah, Gopal, Hina, Irfan, Juhi — sit in a row. Gopal sits exactly in the middle. Juhi sits at the left end. Irfan sits immediately to the right of Gopal. Hina sits between Juhi and Gopal. Find the seating order, left to right.
A) Juhi, Hina, Gopal, Irfan, Farah
B) Juhi, Gopal, Hina, Irfan, Farah
C) Hina, Juhi, Gopal, Irfan, Farah
D) Juhi, Hina, Irfan, Gopal, Farah

**Q6.** Sanjana walks 15 m North, then 9 m further North, then 6 m South. How far is she from her starting point, and in which direction?
A) 18 m North  B) 24 m North  C) 12 m North  D) 9 m North

**Q7.** If the 9th day of a month is a Wednesday, what day of the week is the 26th of the same month?
A) Friday  B) Saturday  C) Sunday  D) Thursday

**Q8.** Find the odd one out: A) 14  B) 21  C) 35  D) 46
(Hint: three of these are multiples of the same number.)

**Q9.** Find the next number: 3, 4, 7, 11, 18, ___
A) 27  B) 28  C) 29  D) 31

**Q10.** In a class, Ramesh is 7th from the top and 21st from the bottom in a rank list. How many students are in the class?
A) 26  B) 27  C) 28  D) 29

## Section 2 — Mathematical Reasoning (Q11–Q20, 1 mark each)

**Q11.** 67,890 + 24,578 = ?
A) 92468  B) 92368  C) 92568  D) 91468

**Q12.** 85,000 − 37,456 = ?
A) 47544  B) 47444  C) 47644  D) 46544

**Q13.** 4,567 × 6 = ?
A) 27402  B) 27302  C) 27502  D) 27392

**Q14.** 7,245 ÷ 15 = ?
A) 483  B) 473  C) 493  D) 463

**Q15.** Find the HCF of 42 and 56.
A) 7  B) 14  C) 21  D) 28

**Q16.** Find the LCM of 10 and 14.
A) 35  B) 56  C) 70  D) 140

**Q17.** Which of these fractions is the greatest: 3/7, 5/8, 4/9, 7/11?
A) 3/7  B) 5/8  C) 4/9  D) 7/11

**Q18.** 5 km 400 m + 2 km 750 m = ?
A) 7 km 150 m  B) 8 km 150 m  C) 8 km 50 m  D) 7 km 950 m

**Q19.** A rectangular field is 36 m long and 24 m wide. Find its area.
A) 844 sq m  B) 854 sq m  C) 864 sq m  D) 874 sq m

**Q20.** A regular nonagon (9 sides) has a perimeter of 99 cm. Find the length of each side.
A) 9 cm  B) 10 cm  C) 11 cm  D) 12 cm

## Section 3 — Everyday Mathematics (Q21–Q30, 1 mark each)

**Q21.** A school collected ₹76,800 from 6 classes equally. How much did each class contribute?
A) ₹12,600  B) ₹12,700  C) ₹12,800  D) ₹12,900

**Q22.** A factory makes 1,800 toys in 6 hours, at a steady rate. How many toys does it make in 7 hours?
A) 2,000  B) 2,050  C) 2,100  D) 2,150

**Q23.** A tank holds 2,750 L. It is filled by a pipe at 125 L per minute. How long will it take to fill the tank completely?
A) 20 min  B) 21 min  C) 22 min  D) 24 min

**Q24.** A bus leaves city A at 8:00 a.m., stops for 15 minutes on the way, and reaches city B at 12:50 p.m. How much time did the bus spend actually travelling (excluding the stop)?
A) 4 h 15 min  B) 4 h 25 min  C) 4 h 35 min  D) 4 h 45 min

**Q25.** 5 L 500 mL of one liquid is mixed with 3 L 700 mL of another, then poured equally into 4 bottles. How much does each bottle get?
A) 2 L 100 mL  B) 2 L 200 mL  C) 2 L 300 mL  D) 2 L 400 mL

**Q26.** Rohit buys 8 pens at ₹22 each and gets a discount of ₹16 on the total bill. How much does he pay?
A) ₹156  B) ₹158  C) ₹160  D) ₹162

**Q27.** A cistern with 1,440 L of water is used for irrigation at the rate of 1/8 of its original capacity every day. How much water remains after 3 days?
A) 850 L  B) 875 L  C) 900 L  D) 925 L

**Q28.** A delivery van carries 4 boxes weighing 16 kg 400 g, 13 kg 600 g, 10 kg 800 g, and 9 kg 200 g. Find the total weight carried.
A) 49 kg  B) 49 kg 500 g  C) 50 kg  D) 50 kg 500 g

**Q29.** 1,080 pencils are packed equally into boxes of 24 pencils each. How many boxes will there be?
A) 42  B) 43  C) 44  D) 45

**Q30.** A trader bought 70 kg of wheat for ₹2,800 and sold all of it at ₹44 per kg. Find his total profit.
A) ₹240  B) ₹260  C) ₹280  D) ₹300

## Section 4 — Achievers Section (Q31–Q35, 2 marks each)

**Q31.** A number is first multiplied by 8, then 30 is subtracted from the result, giving 90. Find the original number.
A) 13  B) 14  C) 15  D) 16

**Q32.** The sum of three numbers is 168. The second number is twice the first, and the third is 18 more than the second. Find the first number.
A) 26  B) 28  C) 30  D) 32

**Q33.** Divya had a certain amount of money. She spent 1/4 of it on books, then 2/5 of what remained on stationery, and was left with ₹900. How much money did she have at first?
A) ₹1,800  B) ₹1,900  C) ₹2,000  D) ₹2,100

**Q34.** In a school, the number of boys is 5 more than twice the number of girls. There are 362 students in total, and 119 of them are girls. Find the number of boys.
A) 237  B) 240  C) 243  D) 246

**Q35.** A sequence starts with 2 and 3, and each later term is the sum of the two terms before it: 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, ___. Find the next term.
A) 18  B) 19  C) 20  D) 21
