# Sample Paper 5 — Answer Key

Total: 40 marks (30 × 1 mark + 5 × 2 marks), 60 minutes.

## Section 1 — Logical Reasoning

1. **D) Peacock** — Lion, Tiger, Leopard are big cats; Peacock is a bird.
2. **B) Account** — A key opens a lock; a password opens (access to) an account.
3. **A) QEGCP** — The rule is "+2 letters": O→Q, C→E, E→G, A→C, N→P.
4. **C) 37** — The gaps increase by 2 each time (+3, +5, +7, +9, +11): 2, 5, 10, 17, 26, 37.
5. **B) 6** — Students to the right = total − position from left = 10 − 4 = 6.
6. **B) South** — Facing East, a right turn faces you South.
7. **B) Thursday** — 20 − 1 = 19 days later; 19 ÷ 7 leaves a remainder of 5, so count 5 days forward from Saturday: Sun, Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu → Thursday.
8. **D) 33** — 14, 21, 28 are multiples of 7; 33 is not.
9. **C) 64** — Each term doubles: 4, 8, 16, 32, 64.
10. **A) JCRRA** — The rule is "+2 letters" (with Y wrapping to A): H→J, A→C, P→R, P→R, Y→A.

## Section 2 — Mathematical Reasoning

11. **A) 9134** — 6789 + 2345 = 9134.
12. **A) 4436** — 7004 − 2568 = 4436.
13. **A) 1881** — 627 × 3 = 1881.
14. **A) 698** — 4886 ÷ 7 = 698 exactly.
15. **B) 9** — Factors of 18: 1,2,3,6,9,18. Factors of 27: 1,3,9,27. HCF = 9.
16. **A) 18** — Multiples of 6: 6,12,18... Multiples of 9: 9,18... First common one = 18.
17. **A) 1/5, 2/5, 3/5, 4/5** — Same denominator, so order by numerator, smallest to largest.
18. **B) 5000 m** — 1 km = 1000 m, so 5 km = 5000 m.
19. **C) 49 sq cm** — Area = side × side = 7 × 7 = 49 sq cm.
20. **D) Countless (infinite)** — A circle can be folded along any diameter, so it has infinitely many lines of symmetry.

## Section 3 — Everyday Mathematics

21. **A) 1436** — 980 + 456 = 1436.
22. **C) 1200** — 150 × 8 = 1200.
23. **C) 24** — 600 ÷ 25 = 24 reams.
24. **B) 3:05 p.m.** — 11:15 a.m. + 3 h = 2:15 p.m.; + 50 more minutes = 3:05 p.m.
25. **C) 50** — 20 L = 20000 mL; 20000 ÷ 400 = 50 glasses.
26. **B) ₹266** — (3 × ₹32) + (2 × ₹85) = ₹96 + ₹170 = ₹266.
27. **C) 50 L** — 1/9 × 450 = 50 L.
28. **B) 26 kg 50 g** — 14250 g + 11800 g = 26050 g = 26 kg 50 g.
29. **C) 18** — 324 ÷ 18 = 18 rows.
30. **B) ₹149** — ₹999 − ₹850 = ₹149.

## Section 4 — Achievers Section (2 marks each)

31. **C) 151** — Number = (13 × 11) + 8 = 143 + 8 = 151.
32. **C) 1,200** — Let larger = L, smaller = S. L + S = 2000, L − S = 400. Adding: 2L = 2400 → L = 1200.
33. **C) ₹3,150** — Dress = 2/9 × 5400 = ₹1200; remainder = ₹4200; shoes = 1/4 × 4200 = ₹1050; left = 4200 − 1050 = ₹3150.
34. **D) 72** — Green = 7; yellow = 7 + 6 = 13; blue = 4 × 13 = 52; total = 7 + 13 + 52 = 72.
35. **C) 21** — These are triangular numbers, each adding one more than the previous gap (+2, +3, +4, +5, +6): 15 + 6 = 21.
