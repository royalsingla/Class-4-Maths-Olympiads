# Sample Papers

Full-length papers built to the **exact SOF IMO Level‑1 pattern**
(see `../docs/02-exam-pattern.md`):

- 35 questions, 40 marks, 60 minutes
- Section 1: Logical Reasoning — 10 Q × 1 mark
- Section 2: Mathematical Reasoning — 10 Q × 1 mark
- Section 3: Everyday Mathematics — 10 Q × 1 mark
- Section 4: Achievers (HOTS) — 5 Q × 2 marks
- No negative marking, 4-option MCQ throughout

Each paper ships as two files: `sample-paper-NN.md` (questions only — print
or cover the answer key and do it under a clock) and
`sample-paper-NN-answer-key.md` (answers + one-line explanations).

## How to run one

1. Set a timer for **60 minutes**. No calculator, no notes.
2. Attempt all 35 questions. Skip and circle anything you're unsure of
   rather than stalling on it — come back if time remains.
3. Mark against the answer key. Score out of 40.
4. For every wrong answer, write one line in your own error log: *which
   topic, and what the actual mistake was* (silly slip vs. concept gap) —
   this is what makes repeated papers improve the score, not just repetition.
