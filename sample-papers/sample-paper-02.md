# Sample Paper 2 — Class 4 Mathematics Olympiad

**Pattern:** SOF IMO Level 1 — 35 questions, 40 marks, 60 minutes, no
negative marking. Set a timer before you start.

---

## Section 1 — Logical Reasoning (Q1–Q10, 1 mark each)

**Q1.** Find the odd one out:
A) Mango  B) Banana  C) Potato  D) Apple

**Q2.** Doctor is to Hospital as Teacher is to ___.
A) Shop  B) School  C) Garden  D) Kitchen

**Q3.** If each letter of a word is moved 2 steps forward in the alphabet (A→C, B→D, …), BAT becomes DCV. Using the same rule, what does CAP become?
A) ECR  B) EDR  C) ECQ  D) FCR

**Q4.** Complete the series: 3, 6, 9, 12, ___
A) 14  B) 15  C) 16  D) 18

**Q5.** Three children are standing in a line. Kabir is in front of Mira but behind Sana, and nobody stands ahead of Sana. Who is at the very front?
A) Kabir  B) Mira  C) Sana  D) Cannot be determined

**Q6.** Facing East, Ila turns 90° anticlockwise. Which direction is she facing now?
A) North  B) South  C) West  D) East

**Q7.** If today is Sunday, what day was it 15 days ago?
A) Friday  B) Saturday  C) Monday  D) Thursday

**Q8.** Find the odd one out:
A) 11  B) 13  C) 15  D) 17

**Q9.** Find the next number: 5, 10, 20, 40, ___
A) 60  B) 70  C) 80  D) 90

**Q10.** In a certain code, FISH is written as GJTI (each letter moved 1 step forward). How is BIRD written in the same code?
A) CJSE  B) CISE  C) CJTE  D) DJSE

## Section 2 — Mathematical Reasoning (Q11–Q20, 1 mark each)

**Q11.** 5,678 + 4,345 = ?
A) 10023  B) 10013  C) 9923  D) 10123

**Q12.** 9,500 − 3,675 = ?
A) 5825  B) 5815  C) 5925  D) 5725

**Q13.** 456 × 7 = ?
A) 3192  B) 3182  C) 3292  D) 3092

**Q14.** 8,064 ÷ 8 = ?
A) 1008  B) 1080  C) 918  D) 1018

**Q15.** Find the HCF of 16 and 24.
A) 4  B) 6  C) 8  D) 12

**Q16.** Find the LCM of 5 and 8.
A) 13  B) 35  C) 40  D) 45

**Q17.** Which fraction is greater: 3/5 or 1/2?
A) 3/5  B) 1/2  C) Equal  D) Cannot say

**Q18.** Convert 4 kg into grams.
A) 400 g  B) 4000 g  C) 40 g  D) 40000 g

**Q19.** Find the perimeter of a rectangle with length 15 cm and breadth 9 cm.
A) 46 cm  B) 48 cm  C) 50 cm  D) 54 cm

**Q20.** How many lines of symmetry does a (non-square) rectangle have?
A) 1  B) 2  C) 3  D) 4

## Section 3 — Everyday Mathematics (Q21–Q30, 1 mark each)

**Q21.** A vendor had 850 oranges. He sold 375 in the morning and 240 in the evening. How many oranges are left?
A) 235  B) 245  C) 225  D) 255

**Q22.** A car travels 65 km in 1 hour. How far will it travel in 4 hours at the same speed?
A) 250 km  B) 260 km  C) 270 km  D) 240 km

**Q23.** Books are packed 12 to a box. How many boxes are needed to pack 276 books?
A) 21  B) 22  C) 23  D) 24

**Q24.** A movie starts at 6:40 p.m. and ends at 9:05 p.m. How long is the movie?
A) 2 h 15 min  B) 2 h 25 min  C) 2 h 35 min  D) 2 h 20 min

**Q25.** A juice bottle holds 1,500 mL. How many such bottles are needed to collect 9 L of juice?
A) 5  B) 6  C) 7  D) 9

**Q26.** Simran buys 4 kg of apples at ₹60 per kg. How much does she pay in total?
A) ₹220  B) ₹240  C) ₹260  D) ₹200

**Q27.** A tank has 500 L of water. Each day 45 L is used. How much water is left after 6 days?
A) 220 L  B) 230 L  C) 240 L  D) 250 L

**Q28.** The weights of two boxes are 12 kg 400 g and 9 kg 800 g. What is their combined weight?
A) 21 kg 200 g  B) 22 kg 200 g  C) 22 kg 800 g  D) 21 kg 800 g

**Q29.** 18 students are to be seated equally in 3 rows for a school photo. How many students will sit in each row?
A) 5  B) 6  C) 7  D) 9

**Q30.** A shopkeeper buys a toy for ₹150 and sells it for ₹185. How much profit does he make?
A) ₹30  B) ₹35  C) ₹40  D) ₹45

## Section 4 — Achievers Section (Q31–Q35, 2 marks each)

**Q31.** A number is divided by 9, giving a quotient of 14 and a remainder of 5. Find the number.
A) 126  B) 131  C) 135  D) 140

**Q32.** The sum of two numbers is 980. The smaller number is 410. Find the sum of their difference and 100.
A) 240  B) 250  C) 260  D) 270

**Q33.** Karan had ₹2,000. He spent 1/4 of it on a book and 2/5 of what remained on a watch. How much money does he have left?
A) ₹800  B) ₹900  C) ₹1,000  D) ₹750

**Q34.** In a basket, the number of mangoes is thrice the number of apples. The number of oranges is 10 more than the number of apples. If there are 15 apples, how many fruits are there in the basket in total?
A) 75  B) 80  C) 85  D) 90

**Q35.** Dots are arranged in triangles: 1 dot in the 1st triangle, 3 in the 2nd, 6 in the 3rd, 10 in the 4th. How many dots will be in the 6th triangle?
A) 15  B) 18  C) 21  D) 24
