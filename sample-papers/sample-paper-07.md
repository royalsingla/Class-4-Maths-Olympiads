# Sample Paper 7 — Class 4 Mathematics Olympiad (Level‑2 Difficulty Step-Up)

**Pattern:** Same 35 questions / 40 marks / 60 minutes / no negative marking
as Papers 1–6, but noticeably harder throughout — closer to SOF IMO
**Level 2**. Attempt this only after scoring comfortably on Sample Paper 6.

---

## Section 1 — Logical Reasoning (Q1–Q10, 1 mark each)

**Q1.** Find the odd one out:
A) Mango  B) Banana  C) Potato  D) Apple

**Q2.** Pen is to Write as Scissors is to ___.
A) Cut  B) Paper  C) Sharp  D) Blade

**Q3.** If each letter of a word is moved 3 steps forward in the alphabet (wrapping Z→C), BOOK becomes ERRN. Using the same rule, what does GARDEN become?
A) JDUGHQ  B) JDUHGQ  C) IDUGHQ  D) JEUGHQ

**Q4.** This series follows two patterns at once — the 1st, 3rd, 5th, 7th terms, and the 2nd, 4th, 6th, 8th terms. Find the missing (8th) term: 3, 50, 6, 47, 9, 44, 12, ___
A) 39  B) 40  C) 41  D) 42

**Q5.** Five friends sit in a row. Leela sits second from the left. Manoj sits at the right end. Karan sits immediately to the left of Manoj. Omar sits between Leela and Karan. In what order are they seated, left to right?
A) Nisha, Leela, Omar, Karan, Manoj
B) Leela, Nisha, Omar, Karan, Manoj
C) Nisha, Leela, Karan, Omar, Manoj
D) Nisha, Omar, Leela, Karan, Manoj

**Q6.** Rahul walks 10 m East, then 5 m North, then 10 m West, then 5 m North again. How far is he from his starting point, and in which direction?
A) 10 m North  B) 10 m South  C) 5 m North  D) 20 m North

**Q7.** If the 5th day of a month is a Thursday, what day of the week is the 28th of the same month?
A) Friday  B) Saturday  C) Sunday  D) Thursday

**Q8.** Find the odd one out: A) 16  B) 25  C) 36  D) 48
(Hint: three of these are a number multiplied by itself.)

**Q9.** Find the next number: 1, 3, 12, 60, ___
A) 300  B) 320  C) 340  D) 360

**Q10.** In a queue, Vikas is 8th from the front and 13th from the back. If 3 more people join the queue at the back end, what will be Vikas's new position from the back?
A) 13th  B) 14th  C) 15th  D) 16th

## Section 2 — Mathematical Reasoning (Q11–Q20, 1 mark each)

**Q11.** 45,678 + 36,789 = ?
A) 82467  B) 82367  C) 82567  D) 81467

**Q12.** 70,000 − 34,567 = ?
A) 35433  B) 35333  C) 35533  D) 34433

**Q13.** 2,345 × 7 = ?
A) 16415  B) 16315  C) 16515  D) 16405

**Q14.** 8,232 ÷ 14 = ?
A) 588  B) 578  C) 598  D) 568

**Q15.** Find the HCF of 54 and 72.
A) 9  B) 12  C) 18  D) 36

**Q16.** Find the LCM of 9 and 15.
A) 30  B) 45  C) 60  D) 90

**Q17.** Which of these fractions is the greatest: 7/8, 5/6, 11/12, 4/5?
A) 7/8  B) 5/6  C) 11/12  D) 4/5

**Q18.** 3 km 650 m + 2 km 800 m = ?
A) 6 km 450 m  B) 6 km 350 m  C) 5 km 450 m  D) 6 km 50 m

**Q19.** A rectangular garden is 32 m long and 20 m wide. Find its area.
A) 600 sq m  B) 620 sq m  C) 640 sq m  D) 660 sq m

**Q20.** A regular hexagon has a perimeter of 72 cm. Find the length of each side.
A) 10 cm  B) 11 cm  C) 12 cm  D) 13 cm

## Section 3 — Everyday Mathematics (Q21–Q30, 1 mark each)

**Q21.** A school collected ₹68,400 from 4 classes equally. How much did each class contribute?
A) ₹16,900  B) ₹17,000  C) ₹17,100  D) ₹17,200

**Q22.** A factory makes 2,000 toys in 8 hours, at a steady rate. How many toys does it make in 5 hours?
A) 1,200  B) 1,225  C) 1,250  D) 1,275

**Q23.** A tank holds 3,000 L. It is filled by a pipe at 125 L per minute. How long will it take to fill the tank completely?
A) 22 min  B) 23 min  C) 24 min  D) 25 min

**Q24.** A bus leaves city A at 6:50 a.m., stops for 15 minutes on the way, and reaches city B at 11:35 a.m. How much time did the bus spend actually travelling (excluding the stop)?
A) 4 h 15 min  B) 4 h 20 min  C) 4 h 30 min  D) 4 h 45 min

**Q25.** 3 L 600 mL of one liquid is mixed with 2 L 400 mL of another, then poured equally into 4 bottles. How much does each bottle get?
A) 1 L 400 mL  B) 1 L 500 mL  C) 1 L 600 mL  D) 1 L 250 mL

**Q26.** Rohit buys 7 pens at ₹18 each and gets a discount of ₹14 on the total bill. How much does he pay?
A) ₹108  B) ₹110  C) ₹112  D) ₹114

**Q27.** A cistern with 960 L of water is used for irrigation at the rate of 1/6 of its original capacity every day. How much water remains after 2 days?
A) 600 L  B) 620 L  C) 640 L  D) 660 L

**Q28.** A delivery van carries 4 boxes weighing 12 kg 300 g, 15 kg 700 g, 9 kg 500 g, and 8 kg 500 g. Find the total weight carried.
A) 45 kg  B) 45 kg 500 g  C) 46 kg  D) 46 kg 500 g

**Q29.** 945 books are arranged equally into stacks of 27 books each. How many stacks will there be?
A) 33  B) 34  C) 35  D) 36

**Q30.** A trader bought 40 kg of apples for ₹1,600 and sold all of it at ₹48 per kg. Find his total profit.
A) ₹280  B) ₹300  C) ₹320  D) ₹340

## Section 4 — Achievers Section (Q31–Q35, 2 marks each)

**Q31.** A number is first multiplied by 6, then 18 is subtracted from the result, giving 84. Find the original number.
A) 16  B) 17  C) 18  D) 19

**Q32.** The sum of three numbers is 115. The second number is twice the first, and the third is 15 more than the second. Find the first number.
A) 18  B) 19  C) 20  D) 21

**Q33.** Priya had a certain amount of money. She spent 2/5 of it on books, then 1/3 of what remained on stationery, and was left with ₹800. How much money did she have at first?
A) ₹1,800  B) ₹1,900  C) ₹2,000  D) ₹2,100

**Q34.** In a school, the number of boys is 3 more than twice the number of girls. There are 351 students in total, and 116 of them are girls. Find the number of boys.
A) 230  B) 232  C) 235  D) 238

**Q35.** A sequence starts with 4 and 6, and each later term is the sum of the two terms before it: 4, 6, 10, 16, 26, ___. Find the next term.
A) 38  B) 40  C) 42  D) 44
