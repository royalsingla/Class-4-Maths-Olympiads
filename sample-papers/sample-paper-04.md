# Sample Paper 4 — Class 4 Mathematics Olympiad

**Pattern:** SOF IMO Level 1 — 35 questions, 40 marks, 60 minutes, no
negative marking. Set a timer before you start.

---

## Section 1 — Logical Reasoning (Q1–Q10, 1 mark each)

**Q1.** Find the odd one out:
A) Hammer  B) Saw  C) Screwdriver  D) Ball

**Q2.** Pilot is to Airplane as Captain is to ___.
A) Ship  B) Car  C) Train  D) Bicycle

**Q3.** If each letter of a word is moved 2 steps forward in the alphabet, LION becomes NKQP. Using the same rule, what does TIGER become?
A) VKIGT  B) VJIGT  C) VKIHT  D) WKIGT

**Q4.** Complete the series: 7, 14, 21, 28, ___
A) 32  B) 33  C) 35  D) 36

**Q5.** In a row of 7 children, Meera is 3rd from the left. What is her position from the right?
A) 4th  B) 5th  C) 6th  D) 3rd

**Q6.** A boy walks 10 m West, then turns right and walks 6 m. In which direction is he walking now?
A) North  B) South  C) East  D) West

**Q7.** If 1st March is a Thursday, what day is 15th March?
A) Wednesday  B) Thursday  C) Friday  D) Saturday

**Q8.** Find the odd one out:
A) 21  B) 24  C) 27  D) 29

**Q9.** What comes next: 3, 9, 27, 81, ___
A) 162  B) 216  C) 243  D) 270

**Q10.** In a code, PLAY is written as RNCA (each letter moved 2 steps forward, wrapping Z→B). How is GAME written in the same code?
A) ICOG  B) IBOG  C) ICOH  D) JCOG

## Section 2 — Mathematical Reasoning (Q11–Q20, 1 mark each)

**Q11.** 8,456 + 3,678 = ?
A) 12134  B) 12124  C) 12234  D) 12044

**Q12.** 10,000 − 4,567 = ?
A) 5433  B) 5443  C) 5343  D) 5533

**Q13.** 512 × 4 = ?
A) 2048  B) 2038  C) 2148  D) 2008

**Q14.** 6,048 ÷ 9 = ?
A) 672  B) 682  C) 662  D) 674

**Q15.** Which of these is a common multiple of 3 and 4?
A) 9  B) 12  C) 16  D) 20

**Q16.** Which of these is a prime number?
A) 33  B) 37  C) 39  D) 42

**Q17.** Which fraction is equal to 0.6?
A) 1/6  B) 2/3  C) 3/5  D) 3/6

**Q18.** Convert 7,500 mL into litres and millilitres.
A) 7 L 500 mL  B) 75 L  C) 7 L 50 mL  D) 750 L

**Q19.** Find the perimeter of an equilateral triangle with side 11 cm.
A) 22 cm  B) 33 cm  C) 44 cm  D) 31 cm

**Q20.** How many vertices does a triangle have?
A) 2  B) 3  C) 4  D) 1

## Section 3 — Everyday Mathematics (Q21–Q30, 1 mark each)

**Q21.** A stadium has 1,350 seats in the lower section and 980 in the upper section. How many seats in total?
A) 2330  B) 2320  C) 2340  D) 2230

**Q22.** A baker makes 24 cupcakes in each batch. How many cupcakes will 15 batches make?
A) 340  B) 350  C) 360  D) 380

**Q23.** 504 marbles are to be shared equally among 7 children. How many marbles does each get?
A) 70  B) 72  C) 74  D) 78

**Q24.** A bus leaves at 2:35 p.m. and reaches its destination at 6:10 p.m. How long is the trip?
A) 3 h 25 min  B) 3 h 35 min  C) 3 h 45 min  D) 4 h 5 min

**Q25.** A fruit seller has 15 L of juice. He pours it into bottles of 750 mL each. How many bottles can he fill?
A) 18  B) 20  C) 22  D) 25

**Q26.** Vikram buys 2 kg of rice at ₹45 per kg and 1 kg of sugar at ₹38. What is the total amount spent?
A) ₹118  B) ₹128  C) ₹138  D) ₹108

**Q27.** A tank with 600 L of water is drained at 1/6 of its capacity per hour. How much water is drained in 2 hours?
A) 150 L  B) 180 L  C) 200 L  D) 220 L

**Q28.** Two sacks of wheat weigh 22 kg 500 g and 18 kg 750 g. Find their total weight.
A) 40 kg 250 g  B) 41 kg 250 g  C) 41 kg 750 g  D) 40 kg 750 g

**Q29.** 252 chairs are arranged in rows of 14. How many rows are there?
A) 16  B) 17  C) 18  D) 20

**Q30.** A shopkeeper bought a fan for ₹1,200 and sold it at a loss of ₹150. What was the selling price?
A) ₹1,000  B) ₹1,050  C) ₹1,100  D) ₹950

## Section 4 — Achievers Section (Q31–Q35, 2 marks each)

**Q31.** A number divided by 11 gives a quotient of 16 and a remainder of 6. Find the number.
A) 176  B) 180  C) 182  D) 186

**Q32.** The sum of two numbers is 1,620 and their difference is 180. Find the smaller number.
A) 700  B) 720  C) 740  D) 760

**Q33.** Sameer had ₹4,500. He gave 1/5 of it to his brother and 2/9 of the remainder to his sister. How much money does he have left?
A) ₹2,600  B) ₹2,700  C) ₹2,800  D) ₹2,900

**Q34.** The number of red balls in a bag is 3 times the number of green balls. There are 7 more green balls than yellow balls. If there are 5 yellow balls, how many balls are there in total?
A) 48  B) 50  C) 53  D) 55

**Q35.** Study the pattern: 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, ___, 49. What is the missing number?
A) 30  B) 32  C) 36  D) 42
