# Sample Paper 3 — Answer Key

Total: 40 marks (30 × 1 mark + 5 × 2 marks), 60 minutes.

## Section 1 — Logical Reasoning

1. **D) Spoon** — Pen, Pencil, Eraser are stationery items; a Spoon is not.
2. **B) Sky** — A fish lives in water; a bird's natural space is the sky.
3. **A) HQQV** — The rule is "+2 letters": F→H, O→Q, O→Q, T→V.
4. **C) 66** — The gaps decrease by 1 each time (−10, −9, −8, −7): 100, 90, 81, 73, 66.
5. **D) Uma** — Order: Uma > Sana > Puja > Rita, with Tina the shortest overall.
6. **A) North** — Turning 180° always faces you the opposite way: South → North.
7. **B) Thursday** — Sunday (1st), Monday (2nd), Tuesday (3rd), Wednesday (4th), Thursday (5th).
8. **C) 15** — 4, 9, 16 are perfect squares (2², 3², 4²); 15 is not.
9. **C) 13** — Fibonacci pattern: each term is the sum of the two before it (5+8=13).
10. **A) UCPF** — The rule is "+2 letters": S→U, A→C, N→P, D→F.

## Section 2 — Mathematical Reasoning

11. **A) 10112** — 7245 + 2867 = 10112.
12. **A) 3255** — 6003 − 2748 = 3255.
13. **A) 1945** — 389 × 5 = 1945.
14. **A) 828** — 5796 ÷ 7 = 828 exactly.
15. **C) 24** — 24 ÷ 4 = 6 and 24 ÷ 6 = 4, so it's a multiple of both. (16 isn't a multiple of 6; 18 isn't a multiple of 4; 30 isn't a multiple of 4.)
16. **B) 2/3** — The greatest common factor of 10 and 15 is 5; 10÷5=2, 15÷5=3.
17. **B) 0.75** — 3/4 = 0.75.
18. **A) 6 km 200 m** — 6200 m = 6000 m + 200 m = 6 km 200 m.
19. **C) 80 sq cm** — Area = length × breadth = 10 × 8 = 80 sq cm.
20. **D) 12** — A cube has 12 edges.

## Section 3 — Everyday Mathematics

21. **A) 2121** — 1245 + 876 = 2121.
22. **B) 810** — 18 × 45 = 810.
23. **B) 125 cm** — 750 ÷ 6 = 125 cm.
24. **B) 10:25 a.m.** — 5:50 a.m. + 4 h = 9:50 a.m.; + 35 more minutes = 10:25 a.m.
25. **C) 15** — 9 L = 9000 mL; 9000 ÷ 600 = 15 mugs.
26. **C) ₹95** — (5 × ₹12) + ₹35 = ₹60 + ₹35 = ₹95.
27. **B) 60 L** — 1/5 × 300 = 60 L.
28. **C) 1 kg 400 g** — 7300 g − 5900 g = 1400 g = 1 kg 400 g.
29. **C) 8** — 96 ÷ 12 = 8 trays.
30. **B) ₹250** — ₹2650 − ₹2400 = ₹250.

## Section 4 — Achievers Section (2 marks each)

31. **C) 115** — Number = (12 × 9) + 7 = 108 + 7 = 115.
32. **C) 900** — Let larger = L, smaller = S. L + S = 1500, L − S = 300. Adding: 2L = 1800 → L = 900.
33. **C) ₹1,500** — Groceries = 1/3 × 3000 = ₹1000; remaining = ₹2000; gift = 1/4 × 2000 = ₹500; left = 2000 − 500 = ₹1500.
34. **C) 36** — Erasers = 10; pencils = 10 + 8 = 18; pens = 2 × 18 = 36.
35. **D) 21** — Each term is the sum of the previous two: 8 + 13 = 21.
