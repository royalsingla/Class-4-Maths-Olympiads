# Sample Paper 5 — Class 4 Mathematics Olympiad

**Pattern:** SOF IMO Level 1 — 35 questions, 40 marks, 60 minutes, no
negative marking. This is your Level‑1 dress rehearsal — treat it like the
real exam: set a timer, no notes, no calculator.

---

## Section 1 — Logical Reasoning (Q1–Q10, 1 mark each)

**Q1.** Find the odd one out:
A) Lion  B) Tiger  C) Leopard  D) Peacock

**Q2.** Key is to Lock as Password is to ___.
A) Computer  B) Account  C) Door  D) Car

**Q3.** If each letter of a word is moved 2 steps forward in the alphabet, RIVER becomes TKXGT. Using the same rule, what does OCEAN become?
A) QEGCP  B) QDGCP  C) QEGCQ  D) PEGCP

**Q4.** Complete the series: 2, 5, 10, 17, 26, ___
A) 35  B) 36  C) 37  D) 38

**Q5.** In a row of 10 students, Vivek is 4th from the left. How many students are to his right?
A) 5  B) 6  C) 7  D) 4

**Q6.** A girl walks 7 m East, then turns right and walks 5 m. In which direction is she walking now?
A) North  B) South  C) East  D) West

**Q7.** If 1st June is a Saturday, what day is 20th June?
A) Wednesday  B) Thursday  C) Friday  D) Saturday

**Q8.** Find the odd one out:
A) 14  B) 21  C) 28  D) 33

**Q9.** What comes next: 4, 8, 16, 32, ___
A) 48  B) 56  C) 64  D) 72

**Q10.** In a code, SMILE is written as UOKNG (each letter moved 2 steps forward). How is HAPPY written in the same code?
A) JCRRA  B) JBRRA  C) JCRRZ  D) KCRRA

## Section 2 — Mathematical Reasoning (Q11–Q20, 1 mark each)

**Q11.** 6,789 + 2,345 = ?
A) 9134  B) 9124  C) 9234  D) 9044

**Q12.** 7,004 − 2,568 = ?
A) 4436  B) 4446  C) 4346  D) 4536

**Q13.** 627 × 3 = ?
A) 1881  B) 1871  C) 1981  D) 1861

**Q14.** 4,886 ÷ 7 = ?
A) 698  B) 688  C) 708  D) 696

**Q15.** Find the HCF of 18 and 27.
A) 6  B) 9  C) 3  D) 18

**Q16.** Find the LCM of 6 and 9.
A) 18  B) 27  C) 36  D) 54

**Q17.** Arrange in ascending order: 2/5, 1/5, 4/5, 3/5
A) 1/5, 2/5, 3/5, 4/5  B) 4/5, 3/5, 2/5, 1/5  C) 1/5, 3/5, 2/5, 4/5  D) 2/5, 1/5, 4/5, 3/5

**Q18.** Convert 5 km into metres.
A) 500 m  B) 5000 m  C) 50 m  D) 50000 m

**Q19.** Find the area of a square with side 7 cm.
A) 14 sq cm  B) 28 sq cm  C) 49 sq cm  D) 56 sq cm

**Q20.** How many lines of symmetry does a circle have?
A) 1  B) 2  C) 4  D) Countless (infinite)

## Section 3 — Everyday Mathematics (Q21–Q30, 1 mark each)

**Q21.** A theatre has 980 seats on the ground floor and 456 in the balcony. How many seats are there in total?
A) 1436  B) 1426  C) 1446  D) 1336

**Q22.** A printer prints 150 pages in a minute. How many pages will it print in 8 minutes?
A) 1100  B) 1150  C) 1200  D) 1250

**Q23.** 600 sheets of paper are packed into reams of 25. How many reams are made?
A) 20  B) 22  C) 24  D) 26

**Q24.** A flight departs at 11:15 a.m. and lands after 3 hours 50 minutes. At what time does it land?
A) 2:55 p.m.  B) 3:05 p.m.  C) 3:15 p.m.  D) 2:45 p.m.

**Q25.** A water dispenser holds 20 L. If each glass holds 400 mL, how many full glasses can it fill?
A) 40  B) 45  C) 50  D) 55

**Q26.** Deepa buys 3 kg of flour at ₹32 per kg and 2 kg of lentils at ₹85 per kg. What is the total amount spent?
A) ₹256  B) ₹266  C) ₹276  D) ₹286

**Q27.** A tank with 450 L loses 1/9 of its water through a leak. How much water is lost?
A) 40 L  B) 45 L  C) 50 L  D) 55 L

**Q28.** Two boxes of vegetables weigh 14 kg 250 g and 11 kg 800 g. Find the total weight.
A) 25 kg 950 g  B) 26 kg 50 g  C) 26 kg 150 g  D) 25 kg 50 g

**Q29.** 324 students are seated in rows of 18 for an exam. How many rows are needed?
A) 16  B) 17  C) 18  D) 20

**Q30.** A shopkeeper bought a watch for ₹850 and sold it for ₹999. Find his profit.
A) ₹139  B) ₹149  C) ₹159  D) ₹129

## Section 4 — Achievers Section (Q31–Q35, 2 marks each)

**Q31.** A number divided by 13 gives a quotient of 11 and a remainder of 8. Find the number.
A) 143  B) 147  C) 151  D) 155

**Q32.** The sum of two numbers is 2,000 and their difference is 400. Find the larger number.
A) 1,100  B) 1,150  C) 1,200  D) 1,250

**Q33.** Farah had ₹5,400. She spent 2/9 of it on a dress and 1/4 of the remainder on shoes. How much does she have left?
A) ₹2,950  B) ₹3,050  C) ₹3,150  D) ₹3,250

**Q34.** The number of blue marbles is 4 times the number of yellow marbles. There are 6 more yellow marbles than green marbles. If there are 7 green marbles, find the total number of marbles.
A) 65  B) 68  C) 70  D) 72

**Q35.** Study the pattern: 1, 3, 6, 10, 15, ___. Find the next number.
A) 18  B) 20  C) 21  D) 24
