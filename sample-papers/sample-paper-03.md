# Sample Paper 3 — Class 4 Mathematics Olympiad

**Pattern:** SOF IMO Level 1 — 35 questions, 40 marks, 60 minutes, no
negative marking. Set a timer before you start.

---

## Section 1 — Logical Reasoning (Q1–Q10, 1 mark each)

**Q1.** Find the odd one out:
A) Pen  B) Pencil  C) Eraser  D) Spoon

**Q2.** Fish is to Water as Bird is to ___.
A) Nest  B) Sky  C) Tree  D) Cage

**Q3.** If each letter of a word is moved 2 steps forward in the alphabet, HAND becomes JCPF. Using the same rule, what does FOOT become?
A) HQQV  B) HPQV  C) GQQV  D) HQQW

**Q4.** Complete the series: 100, 90, 81, 73, ___
A) 64  B) 65  C) 66  D) 67

**Q5.** Among five friends, Puja is taller than Rita but shorter than Sana. Tina is the shortest of all. Uma is taller than Sana. Who is the tallest?
A) Puja  B) Sana  C) Tina  D) Uma

**Q6.** Facing South, Rohan turns 180°. Which direction is he facing now?
A) North  B) South  C) East  D) West

**Q7.** If a week starts on Sunday, what is the 5th day of the week?
A) Wednesday  B) Thursday  C) Friday  D) Saturday

**Q8.** Find the odd one out:
A) 4  B) 9  C) 15  D) 16

**Q9.** What comes next: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, ___
A) 11  B) 12  C) 13  D) 14

**Q10.** In a code, GOLD is written as IQNF (each letter moved 2 steps forward). How is SAND written in the same code?
A) UCPF  B) UBPF  C) TCPF  D) UCPG

## Section 2 — Mathematical Reasoning (Q11–Q20, 1 mark each)

**Q11.** 7,245 + 2,867 = ?
A) 10112  B) 10102  C) 10012  D) 10122

**Q12.** 6,003 − 2,748 = ?
A) 3255  B) 3245  C) 3355  D) 3265

**Q13.** 389 × 5 = ?
A) 1945  B) 1935  C) 1955  D) 1845

**Q14.** 5,796 ÷ 7 = ?
A) 828  B) 818  C) 838  D) 826

**Q15.** Which of these is a multiple of both 4 and 6?
A) 16  B) 18  C) 24  D) 30

**Q16.** Simplify 10/15 to its lowest terms.
A) 1/2  B) 2/3  C) 3/5  D) 5/6

**Q17.** Which decimal equals 3/4?
A) 0.34  B) 0.75  C) 0.43  D) 0.57

**Q18.** Convert 6,200 m into kilometres and metres.
A) 6 km 200 m  B) 62 km  C) 6 km 20 m  D) 600 km 2 m

**Q19.** Find the area of a rectangle with length 10 cm and breadth 8 cm.
A) 18 sq cm  B) 40 sq cm  C) 80 sq cm  D) 90 sq cm

**Q20.** How many edges does a cube have?
A) 6  B) 8  C) 10  D) 12

## Section 3 — Everyday Mathematics (Q21–Q30, 1 mark each)

**Q21.** A library has 1,245 story books and 876 fact books. How many books does it have in total?
A) 2121  B) 2111  C) 2021  D) 2131

**Q22.** A factory packs 18 biscuits in a packet. How many biscuits are needed to make 45 packets?
A) 800  B) 810  C) 820  D) 790

**Q23.** A rope of length 750 cm is cut into 6 equal pieces. What is the length of each piece?
A) 120 cm  B) 125 cm  C) 130 cm  D) 115 cm

**Q24.** A train departs at 5:50 a.m. and the journey takes 4 hours 35 minutes. At what time does it arrive?
A) 10:15 a.m.  B) 10:25 a.m.  C) 10:35 a.m.  D) 9:25 a.m.

**Q25.** A bucket holds 9 L of water. If it is filled using a 600 mL mug, how many mugs are needed to fill it completely?
A) 12  B) 14  C) 15  D) 18

**Q26.** Aditi buys 5 chocolates at ₹12 each and 1 ice cream for ₹35. How much does she spend in total?
A) ₹85  B) ₹90  C) ₹95  D) ₹100

**Q27.** A water tank loses 1/5 of its water each day due to a leak. If it starts with 300 L, how much water is lost on the first day?
A) 50 L  B) 60 L  C) 70 L  D) 75 L

**Q28.** Two parcels weigh 7 kg 300 g and 5 kg 900 g. What is the difference in their weights?
A) 1 kg 200 g  B) 1 kg 300 g  C) 1 kg 400 g  D) 1 kg 500 g

**Q29.** A farmer has placed 96 eggs equally into trays of 12. How many trays did he use?
A) 6  B) 7  C) 8  D) 9

**Q30.** A shopkeeper bought a bicycle for ₹2,400 and sold it for ₹2,650. Find his profit.
A) ₹200  B) ₹250  C) ₹300  D) ₹225

## Section 4 — Achievers Section (Q31–Q35, 2 marks each)

**Q31.** A number divided by 12 gives a quotient of 9 and a remainder of 7. Find the number.
A) 108  B) 112  C) 115  D) 119

**Q32.** The sum of two numbers is 1,500 and their difference is 300. Find the larger number.
A) 800  B) 850  C) 900  D) 950

**Q33.** Neha had ₹3,000. She spent 1/3 of it on groceries and 1/4 of what remained on a gift. How much is she left with?
A) ₹1,250  B) ₹1,400  C) ₹1,500  D) ₹1,600

**Q34.** The number of pens in a box is twice the number of pencils. There are 8 more pencils than erasers. If there are 10 erasers, how many pens are there?
A) 28  B) 32  C) 36  D) 40

**Q35.** In a pattern, each term is the sum of the two terms before it: 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, ___. Find the next term.
A) 18  B) 19  C) 20  D) 21
