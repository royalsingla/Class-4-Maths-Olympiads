# Sample Paper 4 — Answer Key

Total: 40 marks (30 × 1 mark + 5 × 2 marks), 60 minutes.

## Section 1 — Logical Reasoning

1. **D) Ball** — Hammer, Saw, Screwdriver are tools; a Ball is not.
2. **A) Ship** — A pilot commands an airplane; a captain commands a ship.
3. **A) VKIGT** — The rule is "+2 letters": T→V, I→K, G→I, E→G, R→T.
4. **C) 35** — The pattern adds 7 each time: 7, 14, 21, 28, 35.
5. **B) 5th** — Position from right = total − position from left + 1 = 7 − 3 + 1 = 5.
6. **A) North** — Facing West, a right turn faces you North.
7. **B) Thursday** — 15 − 1 = 14 days later = exactly 2 weeks later = same day, Thursday.
8. **D) 29** — 21, 24, 27 are all multiples of 3; 29 is not.
9. **C) 243** — Each term is multiplied by 3: 3, 9, 27, 81, 243.
10. **A) ICOG** — The rule is "+2 letters" (with Y wrapping to A): G→I, A→C, M→O, E→G.

## Section 2 — Mathematical Reasoning

11. **A) 12134** — 8456 + 3678 = 12134.
12. **A) 5433** — 10000 − 4567 = 5433.
13. **A) 2048** — 512 × 4 = 2048.
14. **A) 672** — 6048 ÷ 9 = 672 exactly.
15. **B) 12** — 12 is divisible by both 3 and 4. (9 isn't divisible by 4; 16 isn't by 3; 20 isn't by 3.)
16. **B) 37** — 37 has no factors besides 1 and itself. (33=3×11, 39=3×13, 42=2×21.)
17. **C) 3/5** — 3/5 = 0.6.
18. **A) 7 L 500 mL** — 7500 mL = 7000 mL + 500 mL = 7 L 500 mL.
19. **B) 33 cm** — An equilateral triangle has 3 equal sides: 3 × 11 = 33 cm.
20. **B) 3** — A triangle has 3 vertices (corners).

## Section 3 — Everyday Mathematics

21. **A) 2330** — 1350 + 980 = 2330.
22. **C) 360** — 24 × 15 = 360.
23. **B) 72** — 504 ÷ 7 = 72.
24. **B) 3 h 35 min** — 2:35 p.m. to 6:35 p.m. would be 4 h, but the bus arrives 25 minutes earlier at 6:10 p.m., so the trip is 4 h − 25 min = 3 h 35 min.
25. **B) 20** — 15 L = 15000 mL; 15000 ÷ 750 = 20 bottles.
26. **B) ₹128** — (2 × ₹45) + ₹38 = ₹90 + ₹38 = ₹128.
27. **C) 200 L** — 1/6 × 600 = 100 L per hour; in 2 hours = 200 L.
28. **B) 41 kg 250 g** — 22500 g + 18750 g = 41250 g = 41 kg 250 g.
29. **C) 18** — 252 ÷ 14 = 18 rows.
30. **B) ₹1,050** — ₹1200 − ₹150 = ₹1050.

## Section 4 — Achievers Section (2 marks each)

31. **C) 182** — Number = (11 × 16) + 6 = 176 + 6 = 182.
32. **B) 720** — Let larger = L, smaller = S. L + S = 1620, L − S = 180. Adding: 2L = 1800 → L = 900, so S = 720.
33. **C) ₹2,800** — To brother = 1/5 × 4500 = ₹900; remaining = ₹3600; to sister = 2/9 × 3600 = ₹800; left = 3600 − 800 = ₹2800.
34. **C) 53** — Yellow = 5; green = 5 + 7 = 12; red = 3 × 12 = 36; total = 5 + 12 + 36 = 53.
35. **C) 36** — These are square numbers (1², 2², 3², 4², 5², 6², 7²); the missing one is 6² = 36.
