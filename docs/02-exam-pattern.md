# Exam Pattern — Class 4 Mathematics Olympiad

## 1. SOF IMO — Level 1 (the exam everyone takes)

| Section | # Questions | Marks each | Total marks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Logical Reasoning | 10 | 1 | 10 |
| 2. Mathematical Reasoning | 10 | 1 | 10 |
| 3. Everyday Mathematics | 10 | 1 | 10 |
| 4. Achievers Section (HOTS) | 5 | 2 | 10 |
| **Total** | **35** | — | **40** |

- **Duration:** 60 minutes.
- **Question type:** Objective, 4-option MCQ.
- **Negative marking:** None.
- **Difficulty shape:** Sections 1–3 are mostly single-step/direct; the
  Achievers section is deliberately 2–3 step (HOTS) and worth double marks —
  it's the section that separates Level‑2 qualifiers.

## 2. SOF IMO — Level 2 (for qualifiers only)

- Same 4-section structure and 60-minute duration, but materially harder —
  more multi-step Achievers-style reasoning throughout, including in
  Sections 2 and 3.
- **Who qualifies for Level 2** (any one of these, class-wise/zone-wise):
  - Top 5% of students in that class, zone-wise, in Level 1.
  - Top 25 rank holders, zone-wise and class-wise.
  - The class topper, if ≥10 students from that class appeared and the
    topper scored ≥50%.
- **2025–26 Level‑2 cutoff for Class 4:** 30/40 total, with section minimums
  around Achievers 8, Mathematical Reasoning 6, Everyday Mathematics 9,
  Logical Reasoning 7 (cutoffs are revised each year — treat as a target,
  not a guarantee).
- **Tie-break order** when totals are equal: Achievers → Mathematical
  Reasoning → Everyday Mathematics → Logical Reasoning. This is *why* the
  practice plan gives the Achievers section disproportionate attention —
  it's the highest-leverage section twice over (double marks, and first
  tie-breaker).

## 3. Silverzone iOM — Class 4

| Unit | # Questions | Marks each | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Numbers and Their Operations | 9 | 2 | 18 |
| Parts and Wholes (fractions/decimals) | 4 | 2 | 8 |
| Money, Time and Calendar | 2 | 2 | 4 |
| Geometry | 3 | 2 | 6 |
| Area and Its Boundary (perimeter/area) | 4 | 2 | 8 |
| Graphical Representation of Data | 2 | 2 | 4 |
| **Maths total** | **24** | | **48** |

Plus a **Reasoning and Aptitude** section and a **Scholar's Zone** (HOTS)
section bring the paper to 30 questions overall. No negative marking.
English only.

## 4. Unified Council UIMO — Classes 3–5

| Section | Marks |
|---|---|
| Mathematics | 35 |
| Logical Reasoning | 10 |
| Critical Thinking (HOTS) | 5 |
| **Total (50 questions)** | **50** |

Objective MCQs, no negative marking.

## 5. What this means for practice

All three exams are strictly timed, all-MCQ, zero-negative-marking papers
with the same four ingredients in different proportions: **pure arithmetic
→ applied word problems → reasoning puzzles → multi-step HOTS**. So the
practice plan (`03-practice-plan.md`) and every sample paper here is built
to the **SOF IMO shape** (35 Q / 40 marks / 60 min, 4 sections) since it's
the strictest superset — anyone comfortable with IMO Level 1 is comfortable
with iOM and UIMO's papers too.
